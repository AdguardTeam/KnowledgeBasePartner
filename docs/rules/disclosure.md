## Affiliate disclosure: tell your audience that you earn a commission

Wherever you post an AdGuard [affiliate link](/tracking/affiliate-links) or [promo code](/tracking/promo-codes), tell your audience that you may earn a commission. It’s required by the Terms and Conditions and by advertising laws in many countries.

## What a disclosure must do

- Be easy to notice: not hidden in small print or at the very bottom of the page
- Say clearly that you may earn a commission if people buy through your links
- Follow the advertising rules that apply to you, such as the FTC Endorsement Guides in the US

For example:

- “As an AdGuard Affiliate, I earn from qualifying purchases.”
- “Ad. Affiliate link: we earn a commission if you buy through it.”

## Where to place it

- **Articles and reviews:** at the top of the page, before your first affiliate link.
- **Videos:** say it in the video, and add it to the description next to the link.
- **Social media posts:** in the text of the post, not only in a comment.
- **Podcasts:** say it before you mention your link or promo code.

Social networks and video platforms may also have their own tools and rules for marking paid content. Follow them too.

## Honest reviews

- Share only your real experience with the product.
- Advertising rules in many countries require you to say if you got the product for free. If you got a [free license](/getting-started/personal-license) from us for a review, mention it.

See [Product claims](/rules/product-claims).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a disclosure for a promo code?

Yes. Disclose your affiliate relationship wherever you share your affiliate link or code.

### Is “link in bio” enough as a disclosure?

No. The disclosure must clearly say that you may earn a commission.

### Can I write the disclosure in my audience’s language?

Yes. Write it in the language of your content, so that your audience understands it.
