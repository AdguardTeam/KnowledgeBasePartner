Everything you say about AdGuard products must be true, accurate, and something you can back up. Honest content builds trust, and customers who get what they expected are less likely to ask for refunds.

## Rules for product claims

- Describe only the features that the product really has.
- Don’t exaggerate the benefits or performance of the product.
- Share only your real experience in reviews and testimonials.
- Keep prices, discounts, and product information up to date. Remove outdated offers right away.
- Always say that a discounted price is a discount, not the regular price.
- Don’t scare people into installing or buying AdGuard.
- Write your own content. Don’t copy it from AdGuard websites or from other affiliates. See [Brand guidelines](/rules/brand-guidelines).

## Examples

| Allowed | Not allowed |
| --- | --- |
| “AdGuard Ad Blocker blocks ads and trackers in browsers and apps on Windows.” | “AdGuard is an antivirus that removes all viruses.” |
| “With my promo code, you get a discount on an AdGuard Ad Blocker license.” | Showing the price with your code applied as the regular price |
| “I’ve used AdGuard VPN for a month. Here’s what I liked and what I didn’t.” | A review of a product you haven’t tried |
| “AdGuard DNS has parental control features.” | “Your phone is infected! Install AdGuard now.” |

## Where to check the facts

- *Promo texts* in *Promo tools*: approved descriptions of each product
- The product pages on adguard.com, adguard-vpn.com, and adguard-dns.io
- The *AdGuard media kits* in *Promo tools*

Prices and discounts change, so check them before you publish, and update your content when they change. For current discounts, see [Promo codes](/tracking/promo-codes).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I compare AdGuard with other products?

Yes, if the comparison is honest and based on facts or your own experience.

### Can I write about a feature I haven’t tested?

Only if the feature is described on our official product pages, and you describe it accurately.

### What happens if my content has an outdated price?

Update it as soon as you notice. Outdated offers confuse customers and [break the rules](/rules/prohibited-practices).
