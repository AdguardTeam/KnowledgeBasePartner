## Coupon websites, deals, and cashback: what’s allowed

Promo codes help your audience save, but they can’t be posted just anywhere. Here are the rules for coupon, deal, and cashback websites.

## What’s prohibited

- Posting AdGuard promo codes, discounts, or deals on third-party coupon or deal websites, coupon extensions, or similar platforms, such as RetailMeNot, Coupons.com, KarmaNow, or Honey
- Sharing leaked or private codes. Use only codes that we issued to you, or official promotions that are publicly available
- Showing the discounted price as the regular price. Always say that it’s a discount

## What’s allowed with our approval

- **Your own coupon website.** If you own a coupon or deal website, you can post your AdGuard promo codes there after our written approval.
- **Cashback, loyalty, and rebate websites.** You can promote AdGuard through them only with our prior written approval. We may set special terms for such promotions.

To get approval, create a ticket in *Support*. Add the link to your website and describe how you’ll show the codes. See [Allowed traffic sources](/rules/allowed-traffic-sources).

## Keep your codes up to date

- Each promo code works for six months. When it expires, remove it and add a new one.
- Check that the discounts and prices on your pages are current.
- Remove or correct outdated offers right away.

See [Promo codes](/tracking/promo-codes).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I post my promo code on RetailMeNot or Honey?

No. Third-party coupon and deal websites and extensions are prohibited.

### I own a coupon website. Can I post AdGuard codes there?

Yes, after our written approval. Create a ticket in *Support* with the link to your website.

### My code appeared on a coupon website without my involvement. What should I do?

Tell partner support right away. Send the link to the page with your code. See [How to contact partner support](/support/contact-support).
