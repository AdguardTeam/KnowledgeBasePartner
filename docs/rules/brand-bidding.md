You can run search ads to [promote AdGuard](/rules/allowed-traffic-sources), but you can’t bid on AdGuard brand names. These rules apply to all search engines and to all platforms with keyword-triggered ads, everywhere and at all times.

## Protected terms

Protected terms are:

- AdGuard, AdGuard VPN, AdGuard DNS, and other AdGuard trademarks
- Their misspellings and variations, such as Ad Guard or adgaurd
- Combinations with other words, such as “AdGuard coupon”, “AdGuard review”, or “download AdGuard”

## What’s prohibited

- Bidding on protected terms in any paid search auction, on any search engine, such as Google, Bing, Yandex, or DuckDuckGo
- Using protected terms in your ad text, ad title, or display URL
- Linking straight from a search ad to AdGuard websites with your affiliate link
- Ads that look like official AdGuard ads or say that they’re from AdGuard
- Dynamic keyword insertion or ad customizers that can add protected terms to your ads
- Using domains similar to AdGuard websites in ads

## What’s required

Add all protected terms, including common misspellings, as negative keywords in all your paid search campaigns. This keeps your ads from showing on searches for AdGuard.

## What’s allowed

Search ads with general keywords that don’t include protected terms. The ads must lead to your own page with original, useful content, such as a review or a comparison. From that page, you can link to AdGuard with your [affiliate link](/tracking/affiliate-links).

For example:

- **Allowed:** the keyword “best ad blocker for Windows” leads to your review of ad blockers.
- **Prohibited:** the keyword “AdGuard discount” leads straight to adguard.com with your affiliate link.

Exceptions are possible only with our prior written permission. To ask for one, [contact partner support](/support/contact-support).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I use “AdGuard review” as a keyword?

No. Combinations of AdGuard brand names with other words are protected too.

### Do I need to add negative keywords if I don’t target AdGuard?

Yes. Add all protected terms as negative keywords in every paid search campaign.

### Can I link from my search ad straight to adguard.com?

No. Search ads must lead to your own page with original content.
