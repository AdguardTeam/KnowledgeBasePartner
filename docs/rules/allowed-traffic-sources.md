You can promote AdGuard in many ways. Some of them are allowed right away, and some need our written approval first. Everything else that breaks the rules is listed in [Prohibited practices](/rules/prohibited-practices).

## Allowed without approval

- **Your own channels.** Posts on your website or blog, your YouTube or Telegram channel, and your social media pages.
- **Contextual ads** on websites about topics related to AdGuard, such as internet privacy and security.
- **Banner ads** on your website.
- **Native ads**, made to match the content of your website.
- **Search ads** without AdGuard brand names in keywords or ads. The ads must lead to your own page with original content, not straight to AdGuard websites. See [Brand bidding](/rules/brand-bidding).

## Allowed only with our written approval

- Ads in other people’s channels and messengers, such as a paid post in someone else’s Telegram channel
- Ads on social media, including feeds and groups
- Email newsletters
- Ads in your apps, software, or services
- Software, browser extensions, or toolbars that promote AdGuard
- A coupon website that you own. See [Coupon and deal websites](/rules/coupon-and-deal-sites)
- Cashback, loyalty, or rebate websites
- Sub-affiliate networks

Promo codes need approval too. When we issue a code for the campaign you described in your request, that’s your approval for it. See [Promo codes](/tracking/promo-codes).

## How to get approval

1. In the left menu of the Partner Portal, click *Support*, and then click *New ticket*.

1. In *Summary*, enter a short subject, such as “Approval for a campaign”.

1. In *Description*, describe what you plan: the channel, its audience, the ad format, and links to examples.

1. Click *Send*.

We’ll reply in the ticket. Start the campaign only after you get our approval.

## Rules for email newsletters

If we approve your newsletter, each email must:

- Have a working unsubscribe link
- Show a valid physical mailing address
- Have accurate sender and subject information
- Go only to people who agreed to get your emails

Follow the email marketing laws that apply to you, such as CAN-SPAM, ePrivacy, and CASL. Unsolicited bulk email is prohibited.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I post about AdGuard on my Telegram channel?

Yes. Posts in your own channel don’t need approval. Paid posts in other people’s channels need our approval first.

### Can I run Google Ads?

Yes, without approval, if you don’t use AdGuard brand names in keywords or ads, and the ads lead to your own page with original content. Add AdGuard brand names as negative keywords. See [Brand bidding](/rules/brand-bidding).

### Can I promote AdGuard in an email newsletter?

Only with our written approval. Describe your newsletter in a ticket before you send it.

### Can I use pop-up ads?

No. Pop-up, pop-under, push-up, and click-under ads are prohibited.
