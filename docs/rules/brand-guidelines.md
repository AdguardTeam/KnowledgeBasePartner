## Using the AdGuard name, logos, and promo materials

You can use our name, logos, and promo materials to promote AdGuard. But your website, profiles, and ads must always be clearly yours, not AdGuard’s.

## Promo materials you can use

In *Promo tools*, you’ll find:

- *Promo texts*: short descriptions of our products
- *Banners*: promo images for each product
- *AdGuard media kits*: logos, screenshots, product overviews, and press coverage

You can publish them to [promote AdGuard](/rules/allowed-traffic-sources) as a participant of the affiliate program. Don’t change them. Media kits may have their own terms of use on the pages where they’re published.

Don’t copy other content from AdGuard websites, such as articles or product pages, without our written permission.

## How to write our name

Write AdGuard as one word, with a capital A and G: AdGuard, AdGuard VPN, AdGuard DNS.

## Your website and profiles must be clearly yours

- Don’t use AdGuard or similar names in your domain names or social media profile names. For example, a profile called “AdGuard Deals” isn’t allowed.
- Don’t use AdGuard in the titles of [paid search ads](/rules/brand-bidding).
- Don’t copy our design: graphics, layouts, colors, or other elements that can make people think they’re on an official AdGuard website.
- Make it clear that your website or channel is a third-party promotional resource.
- Don’t call yourself an official AdGuard representative or use “official” badges.

In rare cases, we can make an exception for a profile name. Ask [partner support](/support/contact-support) before you create it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I use the AdGuard logo on my website?

Yes, from the media kits in *Promo tools*, to promote AdGuard. Don’t change the logo.

### Can I name my Telegram channel “AdGuard Promo”?

No. AdGuard brand names aren’t allowed in channel or profile names. In rare cases, we can make an exception: ask partner support first.

### Can I translate the promo texts?

Yes. You can translate the promo texts from *Promo tools* into the language of your audience, without changing their meaning.
