These practices are prohibited in the AdGuard affiliate program. If you use them, we can block your balance, cancel your commissions, and close your account. This list covers the main cases. The full rules are in Sections 6–8 of the [Terms and Conditions](https://adguardpartner.com/terms-and-conditions.html).

## Spam and misleading promotion

- Sending unsolicited emails or SMS messages, or spam in social networks, messengers, forums, and dating services
- Incentivized traffic, content lockers, auto-surfing, and other ways to mislead people
- Phishing and other tricks to make people download AdGuard
- Scareware: scaring people into installing or buying AdGuard
- Teasers and ads with clickbait titles
- Deceptive, false, or misleading ads
- Offensive or obscene ads, and ads with content related to race, national origin, gender, sexual preference, or physical ability
- Ads that break any laws

## Ad formats

- Pop-up, pop-under, push-up, and click-under ads

## Brand misuse

- Bidding on AdGuard brand names in paid search. See [Brand bidding](/rules/brand-bidding)
- Using AdGuard or similar names in domain names, social media profile names, or titles of paid search ads
- Using domains that look like AdGuard websites
- Making your website or ads look like official AdGuard properties. See [Brand guidelines](/rules/brand-guidelines)
- Brand hijacking: attracting people with the AdGuard name and then sending them to other products, services, or bots

## Tracking manipulation

- Cloaking, delayed redirects, or other ways to hide the traffic source
- Cookie stuffing, cookie dropping, and forced clicks
- Malware, adware, ad injectors, and software that inserts your affiliate code into other people’s traffic
- Overwriting other affiliates’ tracking cookies or diverting their traffic
- Bundling AdGuard with other products, services, or incentives
- Artificially inflating the number of installs

## Fraud and accounts

- Purchases with stolen payment cards, SMS fraud, and other financial fraud
- Self-referrals: buying AdGuard products through your own link for yourself, your family, your associates, or your business
- Creating accounts by automated means, under false pretenses, or with false information
- Having more than one affiliate account without our written permission, or selling or transferring your account

## Coupons

- Posting promo codes on third-party coupon or deal websites, such as RetailMeNot or Honey. See [Coupon and deal websites](/rules/coupon-and-deal-sites)
- Sharing leaked or private codes that we didn’t issue to you
- Showing the discounted price as the regular price

## Content

- Copying or plagiarizing content from AdGuard websites or from other affiliates, including rewriting it with AI tools
- Doorway pages and websites made only to get search traffic, without real value for users
- Promoting AdGuard on websites or channels with illegal content, such as pirated software, or with hate speech or violence

## Conduct

- Mass reporting or false complaints against other affiliates’ content
- False or malicious public statements about AdGuard, its products, or the affiliate program

## What happens if you break the rules

Depending on the case, we can:

- Hold your payouts while we review your account
- Block your balance
- Cancel commissions earned through the violation, including commissions we’ve already paid
- Close your account

See [Refunds and payout holds](/earnings/refunds-and-holds).

If you’re not sure whether a promotion method is allowed, ask partner support before you use it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I buy AdGuard through my own link?

No. It’s a self-referral. Such purchases don’t earn a commission. To try a product, request a personal license. See [Personal license](/getting-started/personal-license).

### Can I have two affiliate accounts?

Only with our written permission.

### Another affiliate copied my content. What can I do?

[Contact partner support](/support/contact-support). Send us the link to the copied page, a screenshot that shows the affiliate link, and an archived copy of the page, for example, from archive.today.

### Can I use AI to write my content?

Your content must be original and useful. Using AI tools to rewrite content from AdGuard websites or from other affiliates is prohibited.
