## Guides for AdGuard Partner Program affiliates and resellers

This knowledge base explains how the AdGuard Partner Program works and how to use the AdGuard Partner Portal. It’s for affiliates, who promote AdGuard and earn commissions, and for resellers, who sell AdGuard licenses to their customers.

- [Getting started](/getting-started): how the program works, how to join, and your first steps
- [Links and tracking](/tracking): affiliate links, subaccounts, promo codes, and how sales are credited
- [Postbacks](/postbacks): how to send your conversions to a tracker
- [Commissions and payouts](/earnings): rates, statistics, payouts, fees, and invoices
- [Program rules](/rules): where and how you can promote AdGuard
- [Reseller program](/reseller): ordering and managing licenses, discounts, and the API
- [Help and FAQ](/support): common questions, contacts, account settings, and the glossary
