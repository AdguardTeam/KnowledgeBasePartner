## Affiliate networks: promoting AdGuard through Impact or Admitad

You can join the AdGuard affiliate program in two ways: sign up directly in the AdGuard Partner Portal, or apply through the Impact or Admitad affiliate networks.

## Direct sign-up and networks compared

| | Direct sign-up | Impact or Admitad |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where you sign up | In the AdGuard Partner Portal | On impact.com or admitad.com |
| Welcome Boost | Yes | No |
| Automatic monthly tiers | Yes | No |
| Statistics, rates, and payouts | In the AdGuard Partner Portal | In the network |

The Welcome Boost and the automatic tiers are only for affiliates who sign up directly. See [Welcome Boost](/getting-started/welcome-boost) and [Commission rates](/earnings/commission-rates).

## If you joined through a network

- Your statistics, rates, and payouts are managed in the network. Check them in the network’s dashboard.
- The articles in this knowledge base about the Partner Portal, such as payouts and statistics, don’t apply to you.

## If you want to join directly

Sign up at adguardpartner.com. See [How to sign up](/getting-started/sign-up-affiliate).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do network affiliates get the Welcome Boost?

No. The Welcome Boost is only for affiliates who sign up directly.

### Where do I see my sales if I joined through Impact or Admitad?

In the network you joined through.

### Is the AdGuard affiliate program part of an affiliate network?

No. We run our own program, and we’re also available on Impact and Admitad.
