For 30 days after you sign up, you get our top commission rates on every AdGuard product. No minimum volume is required: the boost works from your first sale.

## Welcome Boost rates

| Product | During the Welcome Boost | After the Welcome Boost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AdGuard Ad Blocker | 70% | 50–70% |
| AdGuard VPN | 60% | 40–60% |
| AdGuard DNS Personal | 60% | 40–60% |
| AdGuard DNS Team | 40% | 20–40% |

Last updated: 14 Oct 2026

If you mostly promote AdGuard VPN, see the [AdGuard VPN affiliate program](https://adguardpartner.com/vpn-affiliate-program.html) page.

## When the Welcome Boost starts and ends

The Welcome Boost starts on the day you sign up and lasts 30 days. To see when it ends, click *Rates* in the left menu of the Partner Portal. During the boost, the page says *Your commission rates for the Welcome Boost period* and shows the end date.

## What happens after the Welcome Boost

1. From the end of the boost until the 1st of the next month, you get the rates of the first tier: 50% for AdGuard Ad Blocker, 40% for AdGuard VPN and AdGuard DNS Personal, and 20% for AdGuard DNS Team.

1. On the 1st of each month, your tier is set automatically, based on your total earnings in the previous month. The more you earn, the higher your rates.

See the table of tiers in [Commission rates](/earnings/commission-rates).

## Who gets the Welcome Boost

Every affiliate who signs up directly in the AdGuard Partner Portal. If you joined through Impact or Admitad, the Welcome Boost doesn’t apply. See [Network affiliates](/getting-started/network-affiliates).

## How to make the most of the Welcome Boost

The boost starts when you sign up, so it helps to be ready to promote right away:

- Choose the product that fits your audience. See [Choose a product](/getting-started/choose-product).
- Prepare your first post, video, or article before you sign up.
- Right after you sign up, get your links and request a promo code. See [How to start earning](/getting-started/start-earning).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a minimum number of sales to get the Welcome Boost?

No. The Welcome Boost works from your first sale.

### How do I know when my Welcome Boost ends?

Open *Rates* in the Partner Portal. During the boost, the page shows its end date.

### Do I get the Welcome Boost if I joined through an affiliate network?

No. The Welcome Boost is only for affiliates who sign up directly.

### What rates will I get after the Welcome Boost?

Until the 1st of the next month, the rates of the first tier. After that, the rates of the tier that matches your earnings in the previous month.
