The AdGuard Partner Portal is where you manage your participation in the AdGuard Partner Program. Here’s what each section of the left menu does.

![Left menu of the AdGuard Partner Portal with Affiliate statistics, Promo tools, Payouts, and Rates](https://cdn.adtidy.org/content/kb/partner/getting-started/getting-started-partner-portal-overview-menu.png)

## Affiliate statistics

Visits, downloads, installs, purchases, renewals, and your earnings. You can filter the statistics and group them into reports by date, country, subaccount, referral, product, or source. See [Affiliate statistics](/earnings/earnings-reports).

## Promo tools

Tools to promote AdGuard:

- Website links and installation links. See [Affiliate links](/tracking/affiliate-links)
- Subaccounts. See [Subaccounts](/tracking/subaccounts)
- Promo pages, promo texts, banners, and media kits
- Promo codes. See [Promo codes](/tracking/promo-codes)
- Postback URL for your tracker. See [How postbacks work](/postbacks/how-postbacks-work)
- Your referral link. See [Referral commission](/earnings/referral-commission)

## Payouts

Your current balance, your payout method, the *Request payout* button, and your payouts history. See [Payout schedule](/earnings/payout-schedule) and [Payout methods](/earnings/payout-methods).

## Rates

Your current commission rates, the end date of your Welcome Boost, and the table of tiers. See [Commission rates](/earnings/commission-rates).

## FAQ

Short answers to common questions about the program.

## Support

Your support tickets. To ask us something, create a new ticket here. See [How to contact partner support](/support/contact-support).

## News

Announcements about the program, such as changes to payouts.

## My profile

Your email address and password, two-factor authentication, and account deletion. See [Account and security](/support/account-and-security).

## Reseller section

If you’re also a reseller, the left menu shows the reseller sections too: *Orders and payments*, *Licenses*, and *Resources*.

If you’re an affiliate and want to become a reseller, click *Become a reseller*. See [How to become a reseller](/getting-started/sign-up-reseller).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the Partner Portal the same as my AdGuard account?

No. The Partner Portal has its own accounts, separate from the AdGuard account you use for AdGuard products.

### Where do I find my balance?

In *Payouts*, under *Current balance*.

### Where do I find my affiliate links?

In *Promo tools*.
