Signing up takes a few minutes. Your account opens as soon as you confirm your email address, and your Welcome Boost starts when you sign up.

:::note

Your AdGuard Partner Portal account is separate from the AdGuard account you may use for AdGuard products. Create a new account for the Partner Portal, even if you already have an AdGuard account.

:::

## How to sign up

1. Open the [affiliate sign-up page](https://adguardpartner.com/dashboard/auth/registration/affiliate).

1. Fill out the form:

    - *Username*: up to 50 characters. You can use English letters, numbers, periods, hyphens, and underscores.
    - *Email*: the email address you’ll use to log in.
    - *Password*: at least eight characters. We don’t accept passwords that appear in public databases of leaked passwords.
    - *Account currency*: US dollars or euros. You won’t be able to change it later.
    - *How can we contact you for urgent messages?*: for example, your Telegram or WhatsApp contact.
    - *Where would you like to promote our products?*: the URL of your website or social media page.

1. Accept the Privacy policy and the Terms and conditions. If you want to get AdGuard partner news and special offers, select the checkbox for it.

1. Complete the captcha and click *Sign up*.

    ![Affiliate sign-up form](https://cdn.adtidy.org/content/kb/partner/getting-started/getting-started-sign-up-affiliate-form.png)

1. Open the email we sent you and click the confirmation link.

You’ll see *Email confirmed*. Now you can log in to the Partner Portal.

Before you sign up, read the [Terms and Conditions](https://adguardpartner.com/terms-and-conditions.html). They explain how commissions and payouts work and which promotion methods are allowed.

## Which currency to choose

Your account currency is the currency of your balance and minimum payouts. PayPal and Mellow payouts are sent in US dollars, and crypto payouts in USDT. A balance in euros is converted on the payout day. See [Payout fees and currency](/earnings/payout-fees).

## If you can’t confirm your email

- Check your spam folder.
- If you see *Can’t confirm your email*, the link may be invalid. Click the link in the latest confirmation email.
- If this doesn’t help, [contact partner support](/support/contact-support).

## If you can’t log in

- If you see *We temporarily blocked your account for safety reasons*, wait 15 minutes and try again.
- If you forgot your password, click *Forgot password?* on the login page, enter your email address, and click *Reset password*.

## What to do next

1. Add a payout method. See [Payout methods](/earnings/payout-methods).

1. Get your affiliate links and request a promo code. See [How to start earning](/getting-started/start-earning).

1. Check your rates and the end date of your [Welcome Boost](/getting-started/welcome-boost).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need approval to join?

No. Your account opens as soon as you confirm your email address. Before your first payout, we may review your traffic sources.

### Can I change my account currency after I sign up?

No. Choose the currency carefully when you sign up.

### Can I use my AdGuard account to log in?

No. The Partner Portal has its own accounts.

### I want to become a reseller. Do I use the same form?

No. Resellers apply with a separate form. See [How to become a reseller](/getting-started/sign-up-reseller).
