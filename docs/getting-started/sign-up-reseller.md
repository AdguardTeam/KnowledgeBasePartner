## How to become an AdGuard reseller

As a reseller, you buy AdGuard licenses at a discount and sell them to your customers. To join, send us an application: we usually review it within one or two business days.

## Requirements

We approve resellers who have:

- An established online presence
- A proven track record in software distribution
- A commitment to a first order of at least 50 licenses
- The capacity to support their customers

You don’t need to be a registered business. The reseller program is open to companies and individuals who can sell licenses directly to their customers. If you want to earn a commission without selling licenses yourself, the affiliate program fits you better. See [Affiliate or reseller](/getting-started/affiliate-vs-reseller).

## If you’re new to the AdGuard Partner Program

1. Open the [reseller sign-up page](https://adguardpartner.com/dashboard/auth/registration/reseller).

1. Fill out the form:

    - *Company name*
    - *Estimated monthly sales, licenses*: at least 50 licenses
    - *Username*, *Email*, and *Password*

1. Accept the Privacy policy and the Terms and conditions, complete the captcha, and click *Sign up*.

1. Open the email we sent you and click the confirmation link.

## If you’re already an AdGuard affiliate

1. In the left menu of the Partner Portal, click *Become a reseller*.

1. Fill out the *Request reseller status* form:

    - Your name and the target countries for your sales
    - *Company details*: company name, estimated monthly sales, country of registration, website, company number, and tax number
    - *Language for invoices*
    - *Company address*

1. Click *Submit*.

You’ll see *Your reseller application has been submitted*.

## After you apply

We usually review applications within one or two business days. If we approve yours, you’ll get an email, and the reseller sections will appear in your [Partner Portal](/getting-started/partner-portal-overview).

If we can’t approve your application, we’ll remind you of our key requirements. You can apply again when you meet them. In the meantime, you can join the affiliate program.

To resell AdGuard VPN, see also the [AdGuard VPN reseller program](https://adguardpartner.com/en/vpn-reseller-program.html).

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does the review take?

Usually one or two business days.

### Can I order fewer than 50 licenses?

The standard minimum is 50 licenses per order. In some cases, we can lower it. Email us at [b2b@adguard.com](mailto:b2b@adguard.com) before you order. See [Reseller products and discounts](/reseller/reseller-products).

### Can I be a reseller and an affiliate at the same time?

Yes. You can join both programs, and both appear in the left menu of the Partner Portal.
