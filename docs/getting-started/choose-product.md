## AdGuard Ad Blocker, VPN, or DNS: which one to promote

With one affiliate account, you can promote all three AdGuard products. To get more sales, choose the one that fits your audience best, or combine them.

## Products at a glance

| | AdGuard Ad Blocker | AdGuard VPN | AdGuard DNS |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What it does | Blocks ads and trackers in browsers and apps | Encrypts your internet connection and lets you choose a location | Blocks ads, trackers, and unwanted websites on the network level, on all connected devices |
| Who it suits | Anyone tired of ads and pop-ups | People who care about their privacy online | Families and small teams |
| Commission | Up to 70% | Up to 60% | Up to 60% for DNS Personal, up to 40% for DNS Team |

For current rates, see [Commission rates](/earnings/commission-rates). For each product, you can also request a promo code with a discount for your audience. See [Promo codes](/tracking/promo-codes).

## AdGuard Ad Blocker

AdGuard Ad Blocker blocks ads and trackers, speeds up browsing, and warns about dangerous websites. On Windows, Mac, and Android, it works in all browsers and apps.

It suits almost any audience, especially tech, gaming, and how-to content. The customer installs the app through your link and activates a license in it.

## AdGuard VPN

AdGuard VPN encrypts the connection and lets people choose where they want to connect from. One subscription works on several devices at the same time, and the app pairs well with AdGuard Ad Blocker.

It suits audiences interested in privacy, travel, and online security. Subscriptions renew, so you can keep earning on renewals. If you mostly promote AdGuard VPN, see the [AdGuard VPN affiliate program](https://adguardpartner.com/vpn-affiliate-program.html) page.

## AdGuard DNS

AdGuard DNS is a cloud service that works on the network level and protects all connected devices: computers, phones, routers, smart TVs, and game consoles. It has parental control features that help protect children from adult content.

It suits parents, families, and small teams. AdGuard DNS Personal is for personal and family use, and AdGuard DNS Team is for teams.

A person signs up for AdGuard DNS through your website link and buys a subscription with the same email address. If they then buy AdGuard VPN, you earn on that purchase too.

## How to choose

- Think about what your audience needs. For example, a tech blog fits AdGuard Ad Blocker, a travel blog fits AdGuard VPN, and a blog for parents fits AdGuard DNS.
- Check which platforms your audience uses. AdGuard VPN has browser extensions, and AdGuard Ad Blocker has an app for Android TV.
- Try different products with different subaccounts and compare the results. See [Subaccounts](/tracking/subaccounts).

To describe the products, use *Promo texts* in *Promo tools*. Don’t make claims about the products that we don’t make ourselves. See [Product claims](/rules/product-claims).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I promote all three products?

Yes. One affiliate account covers AdGuard Ad Blocker, AdGuard VPN, and AdGuard DNS.

### Which product has the highest commission?

AdGuard Ad Blocker: up to 70%. AdGuard VPN and AdGuard DNS Personal pay up to 60%, and AdGuard DNS Team up to 40%. See [Commission rates](/earnings/commission-rates).

### Does AdGuard DNS have an app to install?

You don’t share an app for AdGuard DNS. Share the website link: people sign up for the service through it.
