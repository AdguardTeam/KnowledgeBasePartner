## Personal license for affiliates: how to request one

AdGuard affiliates can get a free license for personal use, for example, to try a product before writing a review or recording a video. You request it through partner support.

## How to request a personal license

1. In the left menu of the Partner Portal, click *Support*.

1. Click *New ticket*.

1. In *Summary*, enter a short subject, such as “Personal license for review”.

1. In *Description*, tell us:

    - Which product you need: AdGuard Ad Blocker, AdGuard VPN, or AdGuard DNS
    - The email address of the AdGuard account where we should add the license
    - Optional: links to your website or channels where you promote AdGuard

1. Click *Send*.

We’ll reply in the ticket. We decide on the term of each license individually. We can’t add a license to an AdGuard account that already has an active license.

## Don’t buy through your own link

Don’t buy AdGuard products for yourself through your own link or promo code. Such purchases are self-referrals: they don’t earn a commission and break the Terms and Conditions. Request a personal license instead. See [Prohibited practices](/rules/prohibited-practices).

## For resellers

Resellers can get free trial keys to test products before reselling them. See [Trial keys](/reseller/trial-keys).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the personal license free?

Yes. It’s a free license for your personal use.

### How long does the license last?

We decide on the term individually for each affiliate.

### Can I give the license to my audience?

No. The license is for your personal use. To give your audience a discount, request a promo code. See [Promo codes](/tracking/promo-codes).
