You’ve signed up for the AdGuard affiliate program. Here’s what to do next to get your first sales and your first payout.

## Step 1. Add a payout method

Add PayPal, Mellow, or a USDT crypto wallet in *Payouts*, so that you can request your money as soon as you earn it. See [Payout methods](/earnings/payout-methods).

## Step 2. Check your rates

Open *Rates* to see your current rates and when your Welcome Boost ends. For 30 days after you sign up, you get our top rates. See [Welcome Boost](/getting-started/welcome-boost).

## Step 3. Choose a product

Pick the product that fits your audience best: AdGuard Ad Blocker, AdGuard VPN, or AdGuard DNS. You can promote all three with one account. See [Choose a product](/getting-started/choose-product).

## Step 4. Get your links and a promo code

1. In the left menu of the Partner Portal, click *Promo tools*.

1. Copy the website link or the installation link for your product. See [Affiliate links](/tracking/affiliate-links).

1. Optional: add a subaccount to each link to see which channel works best. See [Subaccounts](/tracking/subaccounts).

1. Optional: request a promo code. A discount helps people decide, and sales with your code are credited to you even without a click. See [Promo codes](/tracking/promo-codes).

## Step 5. Prepare your content

In *Promo tools*, you’ll find materials to help you:

- *Promo texts*: short descriptions of each product
- *Banners*: promo images for each product
- *Promo pages*: landing pages that show the benefits of AdGuard Ad Blocker and AdGuard VPN
- *AdGuard media kits*: logos, screenshots, product overviews, and press coverage

Write about the products honestly, in your own words. Don’t copy content from AdGuard websites or from other affiliates. See [Product claims](/rules/product-claims).

## Step 6. Publish and follow the rules

- You can post in your own channels without our approval: your website, blog, YouTube or Telegram channel, or social media pages.
- Ads on social media, ads in other people’s channels, email newsletters, and ads in apps need our approval first. See [Allowed traffic sources](/rules/allowed-traffic-sources).
- Search ads are allowed, but you can’t bid on AdGuard brand names. See [Brand bidding](/rules/brand-bidding).
- Tell your audience that you earn a commission. See [Affiliate disclosure](/rules/disclosure).

## Step 7. Track your results

Open *Affiliate statistics* to see visits, installs, and sales. Visits appear right away, and installs, purchases, and earnings can take up to 48 hours. See [Affiliate statistics](/earnings/earnings-reports).

## Step 8. Request your first payout

When your balance reaches the minimum for your payout method, request a payout in *Payouts*. We send payouts every Thursday. Before your first payout, we may review your traffic sources. See [Payout schedule](/earnings/payout-schedule).

## Frequently asked questions

### Which product should I start with?

The one that fits your audience. For example, people who read about privacy may be interested in AdGuard VPN, and parents in AdGuard DNS with its parental control. See [Choose a product](/getting-started/choose-product).

### Do I need a promo code?

No, but it can help. A promo code gives your audience a discount, and sales with your code are credited to you even without a click.

### How soon can I get paid?

As soon as your balance reaches the minimum for your payout method. We send payouts every Thursday.
