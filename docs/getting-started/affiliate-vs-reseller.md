The AdGuard Partner Program has two programs. As an affiliate, you promote AdGuard and earn a commission, while customers buy from us. As a reseller, you buy licenses from us at a discount and sell them to your customers yourself.

## Affiliate and reseller programs compared

| | Affiliate program | Reseller program |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who it’s for | Content creators, bloggers, review websites, social media influencers | Software distributors, IT companies, system integrators, value-added resellers |
| How you earn | Up to 70% commission on sales and renewals, plus 5% of the commission earned by affiliates you invite | Up to 80% discount off the retail price. You set your own prices |
| Initial investment | None | First order of at least 50 licenses |
| Payments | We pay you every week on your request | You pay us for licenses: in advance or, for large partners, after a period |
| Licenses | We handle them. You only share links and codes | You get license keys and manage them. Large partners can also use the API |
| Customer support | We support the customers you refer | You provide first-line support. Our technical team helps with complex issues |
| Marketing tools | Affiliate links, promo codes, banners, promo pages | Marketing kit, product descriptions, technical documentation, banners |

Last updated: 14 Oct 2026

## When to choose the affiliate program

Choose the affiliate program if you have an audience and want to earn from recommendations, without selling anything yourself. There’s no upfront cost, and you can start right after you sign up.

To promote AdGuard VPN, see the [AdGuard VPN affiliate program](https://adguardpartner.com/en/vpn-affiliate-program.html). To join, see [How to sign up](/getting-started/sign-up-affiliate).

## When to choose the reseller program

Choose the reseller program if you sell software to your own customers and can provide basic support to them. Both registered businesses and individuals can apply.

We approve resellers who have:

- An established online presence
- A proven track record in software distribution
- A commitment to a first order of at least 50 licenses
- The capacity to support their customers

To resell AdGuard VPN, see the [AdGuard VPN reseller program](https://adguardpartner.com/en/vpn-reseller-program.html). To apply, see [How to become a reseller](/getting-started/sign-up-reseller). For prices and discounts, see [Reseller products and discounts](/reseller/reseller-products).

## Selling under your own brand

If you want to offer AdGuard technology under your own brand, that’s neither reselling nor affiliate marketing. It’s a technology partnership: we provide an SDK that you build into your own product. For a VPN, see [white-label VPN](https://adguardpartner.com/en/white-label-vpn.html).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I join both programs?

Yes. You can be an affiliate and a reseller at the same time. Each program has its own section in the Partner Portal and its own terms.

### Do I need to be a registered business to become a reseller?

No. The reseller program is open to businesses and individuals who can sell licenses directly to their customers.

### I want to earn a commission without selling licenses. Which program fits me?

The affiliate program. You promote AdGuard, and customers buy from us.

### Can I sell AdGuard licenses on a marketplace as an affiliate?

No. To sell licenses yourself, apply for the reseller program.
