## How AdGuard affiliates earn: from link to payout

The AdGuard Partner Program has two parts: the affiliate program and the reseller program. In the affiliate program, you promote AdGuard products, and you earn a commission when people you refer buy them.

This article explains the affiliate program. If you want to buy licenses and sell them yourself, see [Affiliate or reseller](/getting-started/affiliate-vs-reseller).

## What you earn on

You earn a share of our revenue from every sale and renewal made by customers you referred. One affiliate account covers all three products: AdGuard Ad Blocker, AdGuard VPN, and AdGuard DNS.

We don’t pay for installs, sign-ups, or clicks, and we don’t offer fixed payments. You earn only when a customer pays.

You can earn up to 70% of our revenue from a sale. For your first 30 days, you get our top rates with the Welcome Boost. After that, your rates depend on your monthly earnings. See [Commission rates](/earnings/commission-rates) and [Welcome Boost](/getting-started/welcome-boost).

If you mostly promote AdGuard VPN, see the [AdGuard VPN affiliate program](https://adguardpartner.com/vpn-affiliate-program.html) page.

## How it works, step by step

1. You sign up for the affiliate program. Your account opens as soon as you confirm your email address.

1. In the AdGuard Partner Portal, you open *Promo tools* and get your affiliate links. You can also request a promo code.

1. You share your links or code with your audience: on your website or blog, on your YouTube or Telegram channel, or on social media.

1. A person clicks your link. From that moment, they have 365 days to install the app or sign up to be linked to you.

1. The person buys a license or a subscription. The sale appears in *Affiliate statistics*, and your commission is added to your balance.

1. Each time the customer renews, you earn a commission again.

1. You request a payout in *Payouts*. We send payouts every Thursday.

## How we know a customer came from you

The rules depend on the product:

- **AdGuard Ad Blocker:** the person installs the app through your link and activates a license in it. It doesn’t matter whether they buy the license through your link.
- **AdGuard VPN:** the person installs the app through your link, buys a subscription, and logs in to the app with the email address they used at checkout.
- **AdGuard DNS:** the person signs up for AdGuard DNS through your link and buys a subscription with the same email address. If they also buy AdGuard VPN, you earn on that too.
- **Promo codes:** if a customer pays with your promo code, the sale is credited to you, even if they never clicked your link or installed the app through another affiliate’s link.

If a person clicks the links of several affiliates, the last click before they install the app or sign up counts. See [How sales are credited](/tracking/cookie-window).

## What doesn’t count

- Purchases from distributors and other third-party stores
- Purchases you make for yourself through your own link or code
- Purchases made before you joined the program
- Purchases that were refunded

See [Why a sale wasn’t credited](/tracking/missing-conversions).

## How you get paid

You request each payout yourself. We send payouts every Thursday through PayPal, Mellow, a USDT crypto wallet, or a wire transfer. Each payout method has a minimum amount. See [Payout schedule](/earnings/payout-schedule).

## More ways to earn

- **Referral program.** Invite other people to become AdGuard affiliates, and get 5% of the commission they earn. See [Referral commission](/earnings/referral-commission).
- **Promo codes.** Give your audience a discount and get credit for every sale with your code. See [Promo codes](/tracking/promo-codes).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I get paid for installs or sign-ups?

No. You earn only on sales and renewals.

### Do I need a website to join?

No. A website helps, but you can also promote AdGuard on a blog, a YouTube or Telegram channel, or social media.

### Can I be an affiliate and a reseller at the same time?

Yes. You can join both programs. They’re separate sections of the same Partner Portal.

### Is the program run through an affiliate network?

No, we run our own program. You can also promote AdGuard through Impact or Admitad, but then your statistics, rates, and payouts are in that network. See [Network affiliates](/getting-started/network-affiliates).

### How soon will I see my first sale?

Visits appear in your statistics right away. Installs, purchases, and earnings can take up to 48 hours to appear.
