When a person clicks your affiliate link, we remember it for 365 days. If they install the app or sign up during that time, they’re linked to you. Their purchases and renewals are then credited to you.

## The 365-day cookie

When someone clicks your website link, we save a cookie in their browser. A cookie is a small file that helps the website remember the visit. It’s stored for 365 days.

During this time, the person can come back to our website, install the app, or sign up, and they’ll still be linked to you. The cookie works only in the browser where they clicked your link. If they switch to another device or browser, we can’t link them to you.

Installation links work differently. The app downloaded through them already contains your partner ID. See [Affiliate links](/tracking/affiliate-links).

## Rules for each product

| Product | What links the customer to you | When you earn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AdGuard Ad Blocker | They installed the app through your link | When they activate a license in that app. It doesn’t matter when they bought it or whether they used your link to buy it |
| AdGuard VPN | They installed the app through your link | When they buy a subscription and log in to that app with the email address they used at checkout |
| AdGuard DNS | They signed up for AdGuard DNS through your link | When they buy a subscription with the email address they signed up with |

If a person signs up for AdGuard DNS through your link and then buys AdGuard VPN, you earn on that purchase too.

## Which click counts

The last click before the person installs the app or signs up counts. Clicks after that don’t change anything.

For example:

- A person clicks your link on Monday and another affiliate’s link on Tuesday. On Wednesday, they install the app. The sale goes to the other affiliate.
- A person installs the app through your link. A week later, they click another affiliate’s link and buy a license. The sale goes to you.

## Promo codes win over links

If a customer pays with an affiliate’s promo code, the sale goes to the owner of the code. This happens even if the customer installed the app through another affiliate’s link.

Our own public promotions on AdGuard websites aren’t tied to affiliates. With them, the usual rules apply. See [Promo codes](/tracking/promo-codes).

## Renewals

Renewals are credited to the affiliate who referred the first purchase. For a subscription first bought with a promo code, renewals go to the owner of that code.

If the first purchase wasn’t credited to you, its renewals won’t be credited to you either.

## Purchases in the App Store and Google Play

Purchases made in an app through the App Store or Google Play count the same way as purchases on our website, if the app was installed through your link. Renewals count too. See [Platforms and apps](/tracking/platforms-and-apps).

## What isn’t credited

- Purchases from distributors and other third-party stores
- Self-referrals: purchases made through your own link for yourself, your family, your associates, or your business
- Purchases made before you joined the program
- Purchases that were refunded or reversed. See [Refunds and payout holds](/earnings/refunds-and-holds)

If you expected a sale that you don’t see, see [Why a sale wasn’t credited](/tracking/missing-conversions).

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does the cookie last?

365 days. After a click on your link, the person has one full year to install the app or sign up.

### Do I earn on renewals?

Yes. Renewals are credited to the affiliate who referred the first purchase.

### A customer bought a license without clicking my link. Do I earn?

For AdGuard Ad Blocker and AdGuard VPN, yes, if they installed the app through your link. For AdGuard DNS, yes, if they signed up through your link and paid with the same email address. If they used your promo code, the sale is yours too.

### The customer clicked my link, but the sale went to another affiliate. Why?

After clicking your link, the customer may have clicked another affiliate’s link before installing the app. They may also have installed the app earlier through another affiliate’s link, or paid with another affiliate’s promo code.
