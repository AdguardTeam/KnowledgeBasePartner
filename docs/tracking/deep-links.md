A deep link leads to a specific page, such as the page of AdGuard for Mac, not to the home page. On adguard.com, you can turn any page into an affiliate link: add your partner ID to its address.

## How to make a deep link

1. Open the page you need on adguard.com.

1. Copy the address of the page from the address bar.

1. Add `?aid=YOUR_ID` to the end of the address. If the address already has a question mark, add `&aid=YOUR_ID` instead.

For example:

`https://adguard.com/en/adguard-mac/overview.html?aid=YOUR_ID`

You’ll find your partner ID in any of your links in *Promo tools*: it’s the number after `aid=`. See [Affiliate links](/tracking/affiliate-links).

This works on every page of adguard.com, in every language.

## How sales from deep links are credited

The same way as sales from website links. If a person opens the page through your link, downloads the app from our website, installs it, and buys a license, you earn a commission. See [How sales are credited](/tracking/cookie-window).

## How to add a subaccount

Add `&source=` and the name of the subaccount to the end of the link:

`https://adguard.com/en/adguard-mac/overview.html?aid=YOUR_ID&source=blog`

See [Subaccounts](/tracking/subaccounts).

## When to use standard links instead

Some ad blockers and privacy tools remove tracking parameters from links. If you’re not sure that your deep link will keep your partner ID, use the standard links from *Promo tools*.

For the AdGuard VPN and AdGuard DNS websites, use the website links and promo pages from *Promo tools*.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does a deep link work on all language versions of adguard.com?

Yes. Add your partner ID to the address of the page in any language.

### Can I link straight to the purchase page?

Yes. But for AdGuard Ad Blocker and AdGuard VPN, the sale counts only if the customer installs the app through your link. For example, they can download it from our website in the same browser. If the customer pays with your promo code, the sale is credited to you anyway.

### I added my partner ID, but I don’t see visits. What’s wrong?

Check that the link has `aid=` and your partner ID, without spaces or extra characters. If it still doesn’t work, use a standard link from *Promo tools*.
