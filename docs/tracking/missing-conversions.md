If you don’t see a sale or an install you expected in *Affiliate statistics*, go through the checklist below. Most often, the reason is how the customer installed the app or which email address they used.

## Wait 48 hours first

Visits appear in your statistics right away. Installs, purchases, and earnings can take up to 48 hours. If less time has passed, check again later.

## Common reasons

### The app wasn’t installed through your link

For AdGuard Ad Blocker and AdGuard VPN, a sale counts only if the customer installed the app through your link. It isn’t credited to you if the customer:

- Installed the app before they clicked your link
- Installed it from another source, such as an app store page they didn’t open through your link
- Clicked your link on one device or browser and installed the app on another
- Installed the app more than 365 days after they clicked your link

### Another affiliate got the credit

- The customer clicked another affiliate’s link after yours, before they installed the app. The last click before the install counts.
- The customer paid with another affiliate’s promo code. A promo code wins over links.

### The customer used a different email address

- **AdGuard VPN:** the customer must log in to the app with the same email address they used at checkout.
- **AdGuard DNS:** the customer must sign up through your link and buy the subscription with the same email address.

### The license wasn’t activated

For AdGuard Ad Blocker, a sale counts when the customer activates the license in the app installed through your link. If they haven’t activated it yet, the sale won’t appear.

### The purchase isn’t eligible

- The customer bought from a distributor or another third-party store.
- The purchase was made before you joined the program.
- You bought the product yourself through your own link or code.
- The purchase was refunded. See [Refunds and payout holds](/earnings/refunds-and-holds).
- It’s a renewal of a purchase that wasn’t credited to you.

### Your partner ID was removed from the link

Some ad blockers and privacy tools remove tracking parameters from links. If you made your own link, for example, a [deep link](/tracking/deep-links), try the standard links from *Promo tools*.

## Installs not counted

*Affiliate statistics* shows downloads and installations separately:

- *Downloads* are apps downloaded through your link, whether or not they were installed later.
- *Installations* are installs of apps downloaded through your link.

If you see downloads but no installations, people downloaded the app but haven’t installed it yet.

Android apps downloaded from other websites don’t contain your partner ID. For details on each platform, see [Platforms and apps](/tracking/platforms-and-apps).

## How to ask us to check a sale

If none of the reasons above apply, we can check the sale for you.

1. Ask the customer for the details of the purchase:

    - The email address they used at checkout
    - The order or receipt number
    - The date of the purchase and, if they remember, the date they installed the app

1. In the left menu of the Partner Portal, click *Support*, and then click *New ticket*.

1. In *Summary*, enter a short subject, such as “Sale not credited”.

1. In *Description*, add the purchase details and the link the customer used.

1. Click *Send*.

We’ll check the sale and reply in the ticket. Share a customer’s details with us only if they agree to it.

## Frequently asked questions

### The customer says they used my link, but the sale went to another affiliate. Why?

After clicking your link, the customer may have clicked another affiliate’s link before they installed the app. They may also have installed the app earlier through another affiliate’s link, or paid with another affiliate’s promo code. See [How sales are credited](/tracking/cookie-window).

### Why are there downloads but no installations?

People downloaded the app through your link but didn’t install it.

### Why don’t I see sales with my promo code in my subaccount report?

Sales with promo codes aren’t tied to a subaccount. You’ll find them under *No subaccount*.

### I joined through Impact or Admitad. Where do I see my sales?

In the network you joined through. See [Network affiliates](/getting-started/network-affiliates).
