## How tracking works on each platform and app store

AdGuard Ad Blocker and AdGuard VPN work on many platforms. This article explains how installs and purchases are tracked on each of them and which link to use.

## Which installation links you have

| Platform | AdGuard Ad Blocker | AdGuard VPN |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Windows | Yes | Yes |
| Mac | Yes | Yes |
| Android | Yes | Yes |
| iOS | Yes | Yes |
| Android TV | Yes | No |
| Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Opera | No | Yes |

You’ll find these links in *Promo tools*. See [Affiliate links](/tracking/affiliate-links).

AdGuard DNS doesn’t have apps to install through your link. A person signs up for AdGuard DNS through your website link and then buys a subscription with the same email address.

## Windows and Mac

The installation link downloads the app with your partner ID already inside. If a person visits our website through your website link, the download buttons lead to your personal installer too.

## Android

For AdGuard Ad Blocker, the Android link downloads the app file (APK) with your partner ID. App files downloaded from other websites don’t contain your partner ID.

For AdGuard VPN, use the Android installation link from *Promo tools*. There’s no separate AdGuard VPN app file with your partner ID.

For apps installed from Google Play through your link, the install is credited to you, and so are later purchases in the app.

## iOS

The iOS link opens the App Store. We record the click, and when the person installs the app, the install is credited to you. Purchases in the app are credited to you too.

AdGuard apps for iOS don’t have a promo code field. If you share a promo code with people who use iPhone or iPad, ask them to buy on our website and then log in to the app with the same email address. See [Promo codes](/tracking/promo-codes).

## Android TV

AdGuard Ad Blocker has a separate link for Android TV. It downloads the app with your partner ID.

## Browsers

AdGuard VPN has links for Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Opera. Use the link for the browser your audience uses.

## Purchases in the App Store and Google Play

Subscriptions bought in an app through the App Store or Google Play count the same way as purchases on our website, if the app was installed through your link. Renewals count too. See [How sales are credited](/tracking/cookie-window).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do in-app purchases count?

Yes. Purchases through the App Store and Google Play count if the person installed the app through your link.

### Can I share the AdGuard VPN app file instead of a link?

No. There’s no AdGuard VPN app file with your partner ID. Use the installation link from *Promo tools*.

### Why can’t my iPhone audience enter my promo code in the app?

AdGuard apps for iOS don’t have a promo code field. Ask your audience to buy on our website with your code and then log in to the app with the same email address.

### Does AdGuard DNS have installation links?

No. Use the website link for AdGuard DNS. The sale counts if the person signs up through your link and buys with the same email address.
