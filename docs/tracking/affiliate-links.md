Your affiliate links contain your partner ID, so when a person clicks one of them, we know they came from you. You’ll find all your links in *Promo tools* in the AdGuard Partner Portal.

## Where to find your links

1. In the left menu of the Partner Portal, click *Promo tools*.

1. Find the type of link you need: *Website links*, *AdGuard Ad Blocker installation links*, *AdGuard VPN installation links*, or *Promo pages*.

1. Click the copy icon next to the link.

    ![Website links block in Promo tools with AdGuard Ad Blocker, VPN, and DNS links](https://cdn.adtidy.org/content/kb/partner/tracking/tracking-affiliate-links-website-links.png)

Your partner ID is the number after `aid=` in website links, or after `/d/` in installation links.

## Website links

Website links lead to the home pages of our websites:

- *Invite to the AdGuard Ad Blocker website*: `https://adguard.com/?aid=YOUR_ID`
- *Invite to the AdGuard VPN website*: `https://adguard-vpn.com/?aid=YOUR_ID`
- *Invite to the AdGuard DNS website*: `https://adguard-dns.io/?aid=YOUR_ID`

The website opens in the language of the visitor’s browser.

If a person visits our website through your link, all download buttons on the website lead to your personal installer. For AdGuard DNS, the person needs to sign up through your link.

## Installation links

Installation links download the app right away, without a visit to our website. The app already contains your partner ID.

| Product | Platforms |
| --- | --- |
| AdGuard Ad Blocker | Windows, Mac, Android, iOS, Android TV |
| AdGuard VPN | Windows, Mac, Android, iOS, Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Opera |

For example, the link for AdGuard Ad Blocker for Windows looks like this: `https://download.adguardcdn.com/d/YOUR_ID/adguardInstaller.exe`.

Links for iOS open the App Store. AdGuard DNS doesn’t have installation links: use the website link. For details, see [Platforms and apps](/tracking/platforms-and-apps).

## Promo pages

Promo pages are landing pages that show the benefits of AdGuard Ad Blocker and AdGuard VPN. You’ll find their links in *Promo pages*. If you have a promo code, you can add it to the link, and the page will show the discount. See [Promo codes](/tracking/promo-codes).

## Which link to choose

| What you want to do | Link to use |
| --- | --- |
| Write a review or an article about a product | Website link or promo page |
| Show how to install the app | Installation link for the right platform |
| Promote AdGuard DNS | Website link for AdGuard DNS |
| Offer a discount | Promo page with your promo code |
| Send people to a specific page | [Deep link](/tracking/deep-links) |
| Invite other people to become affiliates | Referral link. See [Referral commission](/earnings/referral-commission) |

To see which of your channels brings sales, add a subaccount to your links. See [Subaccounts](/tracking/subaccounts).

## Short links and redirects

- You can shorten your links with a link shortener.
- You can use a redirect on your own domain if it’s instant, keeps your partner ID and the other parameters, and leads to the same page.
- Don’t use cloaking or delayed redirects, and don’t hide where your traffic comes from.
- Don’t use AdGuard or similar names in your domain names.

See [Prohibited practices](/rules/prohibited-practices).

## How to check that your link works

1. Open your link in a browser.

1. In the Partner Portal, open *Affiliate statistics* and check *Visits*.

Visits are clicks on your link from unique IP addresses. They appear in your statistics right away.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where do I find my partner ID?

In any of your links in *Promo tools*. It’s the number after `aid=` in website links, or after `/d/` in installation links.

### Can I use one link for all products?

Each product has its own website and installation links. Use the link for the product you promote.

### Why does my link open the website in another language?

Website links open our website in the language of the visitor’s browser.

### Can I put my link on any website?

Only in the ways the Terms and Conditions allow. See [Allowed traffic sources](/rules/allowed-traffic-sources).
