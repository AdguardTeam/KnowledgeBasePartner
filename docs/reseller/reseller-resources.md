In *Resources*, you’ll find materials to present AdGuard products in your store and help your customers get started.

## What you’ll find in Resources

In the left menu of the [Partner Portal](/getting-started/partner-portal-overview), click *Resources*. Depending on the product, you’ll find:

- **Product description:** texts about the product and its key features. Click *View all product information* for more details.
- **Release Installer:** the latest version of the app with its release date. Click *Copy link to Release Installer* to share the download link with your customers.
- **Media materials:** logos, banners, illustrations, and more.
- **Redeem page:** for AdGuard Ad Blocker, AdGuard VPN, and AdGuard DNS, a page where customers activate license keys or link them to their account. Click *Copy link to Redeem page*. See [License management](/reseller/license-management).

## Setup links for mobile devices

A setup link helps your Android and iOS customers set up AdGuard quickly. When they open it on their phone, it configures their license, filters, and settings automatically.

1. In *Resources*, on the AdGuard for Android or AdGuard for iOS card, click *Setup link*. You’ll see it if this feature is enabled for your account.

1. Enter the *License key or activation code*.

1. Optional: add custom filters, custom DNS filters, and other settings.

1. Click *Create setup link*.

You’ll get a link and a QR code to share with your customer. If you already have a setup link, you can import it to pre-fill the form and change it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I use AdGuard logos in my store?

Yes. Use the logos and banners from *Media materials* to sell AdGuard products.

### Where do my customers download the apps?

Share the link from *Copy link to Release Installer*, or send customers to the official AdGuard websites.

### How do customers activate a license?

They enter the key in the AdGuard app, or use the redeem page link from *Resources*.
