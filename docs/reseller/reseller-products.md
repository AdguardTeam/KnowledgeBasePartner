As an [AdGuard reseller](/getting-started/sign-up-reseller), you buy licenses at a discount off the retail price and sell them at your own price. You can resell the whole AdGuard product line or focus on one product.

## Products you can resell

- **AdGuard Ad Blocker:** licenses for different numbers of devices and periods, including lifetime licenses
- **AdGuard VPN:** subscriptions for different periods
- **AdGuard DNS:** AdGuard DNS Personal and AdGuard DNS Team subscriptions
- **AdGuard Mail:** licenses that differ by term

To resell AdGuard VPN, see the [AdGuard VPN reseller program](https://adguardpartner.com/vpn-reseller-program.html).

For corporate clients, we can issue a master license key for many devices, such as one key for 10,000 devices. To discuss it, email us at [b2b@adguard.com](mailto:b2b@adguard.com).

## Discounts for AdGuard Ad Blocker and AdGuard VPN

The discount depends on the total number of AdGuard Ad Blocker and AdGuard VPN licenses you buy per month:

| Licenses per month | Discount off the retail price |
| --- | --- |
| 50–99 | 45% |
| 100–199 | 50% |
| 200–499 | 55% |
| 500–999 | 60% |
| 1,000–1,999 | 65% |
| 2,000–4,999 | 70% |
| 5,000–9,999 | 75% |
| 10,000+ | 80% |

Last updated: 14 Oct 2026

You’ll see your price per license when you [place an order](/reseller/ordering-licenses). For bulk purchases and volume discounts, email us at [b2b@adguard.com](mailto:b2b@adguard.com).

## Discount for AdGuard DNS

- 40% off the retail price on all AdGuard DNS products, at any volume. There are no tiers.
- One AdGuard DNS Team subscription serves one end client, with up to 2,000 devices.
- For more than 2,000 devices per client, we offer AdGuard DNS Enterprise on individual terms. To enable it for your account, email us at [b2b@adguard.com](mailto:b2b@adguard.com).
- Monthly billing is available at the same 40% discount.
- The AdGuard DNS discount can’t be combined with other discounts.

## Terms for AdGuard Mail

You order AdGuard Mail licenses in the [Partner Portal](/getting-started/partner-portal-overview), like the other products, and see the price per license in the order form. The volume discounts above are for AdGuard Ad Blocker and AdGuard VPN. For AdGuard Mail terms, email us at [b2b@adguard.com](mailto:b2b@adguard.com).

## Minimum order

- The minimum order is 50 licenses.
- Each order is for one product.
- If you want to order fewer licenses of each product, email us at [b2b@adguard.com](mailto:b2b@adguard.com) in advance. For example, we can agree on 10 AdGuard Ad Blocker licenses, 30 AdGuard VPN licenses, and 10 AdGuard DNS licenses a month, in separate orders.

## Selling under your own brand

Reselling means selling AdGuard products under the AdGuard brand. If you want to build AdGuard technology into your own branded product, that’s a technology partnership with our SDK. For a VPN, see [white-label VPN](https://adguardpartner.com/white-label-vpn.html).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I have to sell all AdGuard products?

No. You can resell the whole product line or focus on one product.

### How is my discount for Ad Blocker and VPN calculated?

By the total number of AdGuard Ad Blocker and AdGuard VPN licenses you buy per month. The more you buy, the higher the discount.

### Does the volume discount apply to AdGuard DNS?

No. AdGuard DNS has a flat 40% discount at any volume.

### Can I set my own prices?

Yes. You buy licenses at a discount and set your own prices for your customers.
