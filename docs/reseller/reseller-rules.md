## Reseller rules: territories, support, and payments

As an AdGuard reseller, you sell our licenses to your customers. Here are the main rules to follow. If you have a distribution agreement with us, it may include additional terms.

## Sell in your territory

Your distribution agreement may limit the countries where you can sell licenses. If it does, you’ll see your account territory in the order form.

- Sell licenses only for activation in your territory.
- If many of your licenses are activated outside it, we review your account. The licenses may be blocked, and your account may be suspended.

## Support your customers

You have a direct relationship with your customers, so you provide first-line support: answer basic questions about the purchases they made from you. For complex technical issues, you have direct access to our technical support team.

## Refunds and replacements

You can block a license if your customer asks for a refund. For recent activations, we generate a free replacement key or deduct the cost from your next invoice. We monitor refunds for legitimacy. See [License management](/reseller/license-management).

## Pay on time

- On prepaid, you pay for each order before we generate the licenses.
- On postpaid, pay each invoice by its due date. If you miss a payment, we restrict your account, and you can’t generate licenses until you pay.

See [Prepaid vs postpaid](/reseller/prepaid-vs-postpaid).

## Selling under your own brand

As a reseller, you sell AdGuard products under the AdGuard brand. To offer AdGuard technology under your own brand, you need a technology partnership. Email us at b2b@adguard.com to discuss it. See also [Reseller products and discounts](/reseller/reseller-products).

## If your access is deactivated

If you see *Your reseller access has been deactivated*, email us at b2b@adguard.com for details.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I sell licenses in any country?

If your account has a sales territory, sell only there. You’ll see it in the order form.

### Do I need to support my customers?

Yes, for basic questions about their purchases. Our technical team helps with complex issues.

### What happens if I don’t pay an invoice on time?

We restrict your account. You can’t generate licenses until you pay, but you can still see your orders and pay.
