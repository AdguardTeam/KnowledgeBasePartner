AdGuard resellers pay for licenses in one of two ways. New resellers start with the prepaid model. Large partners with regular sales can switch to the postpaid model.

## Prepaid and postpaid compared

| | Prepaid | Postpaid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who it’s for | New and smaller resellers, fewer than 100 licenses a month | Large partners with more than 100 licenses a month on a regular basis |
| Contract | Not required | Signed distribution agreement |
| How you pay | You pay for each order before we generate the licenses | You generate licenses when you need them and pay one invoice for the period |
| Discounts | Volume discounts from the reseller price list | Individual terms, including higher discounts |
| API access | Usually not available | On request |

Last updated: 14 Oct 2026

## Prepaid

Prepaid is our standard pay-as-you-go model. You place an order, pay the invoice, and get the license keys after we receive your payment. No contract is needed.

You can pay through Paddle: by card, PayPal, or other online methods. Or you can pay by wire transfer: the bank details are in the PDF invoice. See [How to order licenses](/reseller/ordering-licenses).

For the discounts, see [Reseller products and discounts](/reseller/reseller-products).

## Postpaid

With postpaid, you generate licenses when you need them, without paying for each order. We create a zero invoice for each generation to keep track of it. At the end of each month or another agreed period, we send you one invoice for all the licenses you generated.

The postpaid model is for partners with a signed distribution agreement and a consistent history of high sales. It can also give access to the Distributor API. See [Reseller API](/reseller/reseller-api).

To ask about postpaid, email us at [b2b@adguard.com](mailto:b2b@adguard.com).

To resell AdGuard VPN at scale, see the [AdGuard VPN reseller program](https://adguardpartner.com/vpn-reseller-program.html).

## If you miss a payment

If an invoice isn’t paid on time, we restrict your reseller account. You can’t generate licenses until you pay, but you can still review your orders and complete payments.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which model do new resellers start with?

Prepaid.

### Do I need a contract for prepaid?

No. A contract is needed only for the postpaid model.

### How do I switch to postpaid?

Email us at [b2b@adguard.com](mailto:b2b@adguard.com). We offer postpaid to partners with regular high sales.

### What happens if I block a license on postpaid?

If the license was activated within the last 30 days, its cost is deducted from your next invoice. See [License management](/reseller/license-management).
