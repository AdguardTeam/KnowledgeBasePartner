Before you offer [AdGuard products](/reseller/reseller-products) to your customers, you can test them yourself. We provide free trial keys to resellers for this.

## How to request trial keys

Email us at b2b@adguard.com and include:

- Your reseller account email address
- Your reselling plans: where and to whom you plan to sell
- Which AdGuard products you want to test

We’ll reply by email with the details.

## If you’re an affiliate

Affiliates can request a free license for personal use, for example, to test a product for a review. See [Personal license](/getting-started/personal-license).

## Frequently asked questions

### Are trial keys free?

Yes. Trial keys are free.

### Can I give trial keys to my customers?

Trial keys are for testing the products yourself. To sell licenses to customers, place an order. See [How to order licenses](/reseller/ordering-licenses).

### Can I test AdGuard DNS Enterprise?

Email us at b2b@adguard.com with your project details.
