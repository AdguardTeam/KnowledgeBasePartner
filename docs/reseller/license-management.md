In *Licenses*, you see every license key [you’ve ordered](/reseller/ordering-licenses): whether it’s activated, when it expires, and which order it came from. You can also block keys there.

## How to find a license

1. In the left menu of the Partner Portal, click *Licenses*.

1. Use the filters to find the license, for example, by status or duration.

1. To see the details of a license, click its row. The *License details* window opens.

At the top, you’ll see license counters by product. Click a number to filter the list.

![Licenses page in the AdGuard Partner Portal with key counters and the list of licenses](https://cdn.adtidy.org/content/kb/partner/reseller/reseller-license-management-licenses.png)

The list shows the *License key*, *Product*, *Type*, *Status*, *Created*, *Expiry*, *Order #*, and *Comment* of each license. To save the list, click *Export to CSV*.

## License statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| *Not activated* | The customer hasn’t activated the license yet |
| *Active* | The license is activated and works |
| *Expiring soon* | The license will expire within 30 days. Remind your customer to renew it |
| *Expired* | The license has expired |
| *Blocked* | The license was blocked and no longer works |

## How to block a license

You can block a license, for example, if your customer asked for a refund.

1. In *Licenses*, click the three-dot icon next to the license and click *Block*. You can also click *Block license* in the license details.

1. In *Reason for blocking*, explain why you’re blocking it.

1. Click *Block*.

:::caution

Blocking cancels the license immediately, and any apps using it stop working. You can’t undo it in the Partner Portal. If you blocked a license by mistake, email us at [b2b@adguard.com](mailto:b2b@adguard.com).

:::

To block several licenses at once, click *Bulk blocking*, enter the license keys, and add the reason. Expired and already blocked licenses aren’t included.

## Replacement keys

- **Prepaid:** if the license wasn’t activated or was activated within the last 30 days, we generate a free replacement key for the same product and duration. You’ll get an email when it’s ready.
- **Postpaid:** if the license was activated within the last 30 days, its cost is deducted from your next invoice. See [Prepaid vs postpaid](/reseller/prepaid-vs-postpaid).
- If the license was activated more than 30 days ago, it can’t be replaced.

We monitor refunds and replacements for legitimacy.

## How customers activate their licenses

Customers enter the license key in the AdGuard app. You can also share the link to our redeem page: it lets customers activate license keys or link them to their account. You’ll find it in *Resources*. See [Reseller resources](/reseller/reseller-resources).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I unblock a license?

Not in the Partner Portal. If you blocked a license by mistake, email us at [b2b@adguard.com](mailto:b2b@adguard.com).

### Why can’t I get a replacement key?

Replacements are available only for licenses that weren’t activated or were activated within the last 30 days.

### Where do I see why a license was blocked?

In the license details, in *Comment*.
