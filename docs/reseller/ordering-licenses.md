You order licenses in the Partner Portal, in *Orders and payments*. On the prepaid model, you pay each order before we generate the license keys, and on the postpaid model, the keys are generated right away.

## How to place an order

1. In the left menu of the Partner Portal, click *Orders and payments*.

1. Click *New order*.

1. Select the *Product*, the *License type*, and the *Duration*.

1. In *Quantity*, enter the number of licenses. The minimum is 50.

1. Check the [price per license](/reseller/reseller-products), the taxes, and the total.

1. Optional: in *Comment (optional)*, add a note, for example, the name of your customer. It helps you find the licenses later.

1. Click *Continue*.

    ![New order form with product, license type, duration, and quantity fields](https://cdn.adtidy.org/content/kb/partner/reseller/reseller-ordering-licenses-new-order.png)

You’ll see the *Order details* with the invoice number.

If your account has a sales territory, you’ll see it in the order form. Sell licenses only in this region. See [Reseller rules](/reseller/reseller-rules).

## How to pay on the prepaid model

1. In *Order details*, check the date by which you need to pay.

1. Pay in one of two ways:

    - **Paddle:** click *Pay* and pay by card, PayPal, or another online method. This is the fastest way.
    - **Wire transfer:** download the PDF invoice in *Orders and payments* and use the bank details from it. We generate the licenses after we receive and confirm your payment.

1. When we get your payment, we generate the licenses and send you an email with the license keys.

You can also download the keys in *Order details*: click *Download license keys*. To get a receipt, click *Download receipt*.

## On the postpaid model

When you click *Continue*, the licenses are generated right away. You’ll see *These licenses are provided under your postpaid agreement*. Click *Download license keys* to get them.

At the end of the period, we send you one invoice for all the licenses you generated. See [Prepaid vs postpaid](/reseller/prepaid-vs-postpaid).

## Order statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| *Unpaid* | The order is waiting for your payment |
| *Processing* | We’re processing your payment |
| *Paid* | The payment is received. The licenses are generated |
| *Failed* | The payment didn’t go through. If you need help, email us at [b2b@adguard.com](mailto:b2b@adguard.com) |
| *Cancelled* | The order was canceled |

To cancel an unpaid order, open it and click *Cancel order*.

## Invoices

In *Orders and payments*, you can check the status of each invoice, download PDF copies, and pay unpaid invoices.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I order several products at once?

No. Each order is for one product. Place a separate order for each product.

### Can I order fewer than 50 licenses?

The standard minimum is 50. If you need fewer, email us at [b2b@adguard.com](mailto:b2b@adguard.com) before you order.

### When will I get my license keys?

On prepaid, after we receive your payment. On postpaid, right after you place the order.

### Which currencies can I pay in?

Many. Prices and currency are set automatically based on your region.

### Why can’t I place an order?

If an invoice wasn’t paid on time, your account is restricted, and you can’t generate licenses until you pay. If you see another message, email us at [b2b@adguard.com](mailto:b2b@adguard.com).
