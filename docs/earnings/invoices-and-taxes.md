## Invoices for your payouts and taxes on commission

We can prepare an invoice for each of [your payouts](/earnings/payout-schedule), for example, for your accounting. You pay taxes on your commission yourself: AdGuard doesn’t withhold them.

## What an invoice includes

We issue invoices to both individuals and companies:

- One invoice covers one payout. If you need invoices for several payouts, you’ll get a separate invoice for each.
- The invoice shows the payout amount, the payout date, and the invoice number.
- For a company, the invoice shows the company details, including its VAT number.
- For an individual, the invoice shows your full name and billing address.

## How to request an invoice

1. In the left menu of the Partner Portal, click *Support*.

1. Click *New ticket*.

1. In *Summary*, enter a short subject, such as “Invoice for payouts”.

1. In *Description*, list the payouts you need invoices for: their dates and amounts. Then add your details:

    - **Company:** company name, registration number, legal address, and VAT number.
    - **Individual:** full name, billing address, and tax ID, if you have one.

1. Click *Send*.

You’ll see *Ticket #… created*. We’ll reply in the ticket. You can also request invoices for payouts you received earlier.

## Taxes on your commission

As an AdGuard affiliate, you work as an independent contractor, not as an AdGuard employee.

- You pay all taxes on the commissions you earn.
- AdGuard doesn’t withhold any taxes from your payouts.
- Tax rules depend on your country. For questions about your taxes, contact a tax adviser where you live.

See Section 9 of the [Terms and Conditions](https://adguardpartner.com/en/terms-and-conditions.html).

If you get paid through Mellow, Mellow may ask you for your tax details. Add them in your Mellow.io account, or contact Mellow support if you have questions.

## Invoices for reseller orders

This article is about affiliate payouts. If you’re a reseller, you’ll find invoices for your license orders in *Orders and payments*. There, you can check their status and download them as PDF files. See [How to order licenses](/reseller/ordering-licenses).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a company to get paid?

No. Individuals can get paid through any payout method, including a wire transfer. See [Payout methods](/earnings/payout-methods).

### Can I get one invoice for several payouts?

No. We issue a separate invoice for each payout.

### Does AdGuard withhold taxes from my payouts?

No. You pay the taxes on your commission yourself.

### Can I get invoices for past payouts?

Yes. Create a ticket in *Support* and list the dates and amounts of the payouts you need invoices for.
