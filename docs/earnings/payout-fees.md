When we send your payout, the payment system charges a fee. The fee depends on your payout method. The currency matters too: we send most payouts in US dollars, so a balance in euros is converted.

:::note

This article is about fees on payouts. Payment fees on customer purchases are part of how we calculate your commission. See [How your commission is calculated](/earnings/commission-calculation).

:::

## Fees by payout method

| Payout method | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| PayPal | PayPal’s own fees, if any. They depend on your country and account |
| Mellow | 4% of the payout |
| Crypto wallet (USDT TRC20) | The TRON network fee for the transfer |
| Wire transfer | Ask partner support when we set up this method |

Last updated: 14 Oct 2026

The fee is deducted from the payout amount. Payment systems set their own fees, so check them before you choose a method. To choose or change your method, see [Payout methods](/earnings/payout-methods).

## Total and Net in your payouts history

In *Payouts*, every payout in your payouts history has two amounts:

- *Total*: the full amount of the payout.
- *Net*: the amount after the payment system fee.

To keep a copy of your payouts history, click *Export to CSV*.

## Mellow fee

Mellow charges 4% of each payout. For example, from a $100 payout, you get about $96.

Mellow receives your payout in US dollars. When you withdraw money from Mellow to your card, choose the currency of your card. If the currencies are different, Mellow converts the money at its own exchange rate. For questions about this, contact Mellow support.

## Crypto network fee

We send crypto payouts in USDT on the TRON network. The network charges a fee for each transfer, and it’s deducted from the amount. There are no other fees for crypto payouts on our side.

We send the USDT equivalent of the amount in US dollars, at the exchange rate at the time of the transfer.

## Your account currency

Your account currency is US dollars or euros. You choose it when you sign up, and you can’t change it later. Your balance and the minimum payout amounts are shown in this currency.

Commission tiers are always calculated in US dollars, even if your account is in euros. See [Commission rates](/earnings/commission-rates).

## How currency is converted

- **Sales.** If a customer pays in another currency, we convert the amount at the market rate at the time of the payment.
- **PayPal, Mellow, and crypto payouts.** We send them in US dollars, and crypto payouts as USDT. If your account is in euros, we convert the amount to US dollars at the exchange rate on the payout day.
- **Wire transfer.** When you contact partner support to set up a wire transfer, ask which currency you’ll be paid in.

Your payment system may convert the money again. For example, if your PayPal account uses another currency, PayPal converts the payout at its own rate and may charge a fee for it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why did I get less than I requested?

Because of the payment system fee and, in some cases, currency conversion. Compare the *Total* and *Net* columns in your payouts history. For Mellow, the fee is 4%. For crypto, it’s the TRON network fee.

### Why is my payout smaller than my earnings in Affiliate statistics?

*Affiliate statistics* shows your earnings before payout fees. Refunds also reduce your balance. See [Refunds and payout holds](/earnings/refunds-and-holds).

### Can I get paid in euros?

PayPal and Mellow payouts are sent in US dollars and crypto payouts in USDT, even if your account is in euros. You can withdraw the money from Mellow to a card in euros: Mellow converts it at its own rate. For a wire transfer, ask partner support.

### Can I change my account currency?

No. You choose the currency when you sign up, and it can’t be changed later.
