## Affiliate statistics: visits, installs, sales, and revenue

*Affiliate statistics* shows how your links and promo codes perform: how many people clicked them, installed the app, and bought a product, and how much you earned.

## How to open your statistics

In the left menu of the Partner Portal, click *Affiliate statistics*. At the top, you’ll see the totals for the selected period: visits, installations, registrations, and revenue.

## What each column means

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| *Visits* | Clicks on your link from unique IP addresses |
| *Downloads* | Apps downloaded through your link, whether or not they were installed later |
| *Installations* | Installs of apps downloaded through your link |
| *Registrations* | Sign-ups on our websites and in our apps through your link |
| *Purchases* | Purchases that earn you a commission |
| *Expirations* | Expired trials and licenses |
| *Renewals* | License renewals, automatic and manual, and upgrades |
| *Revenue* | Your earnings, based on your rate |

When a purchase counts:

- **AdGuard Ad Blocker and AdGuard VPN:** when a license or subscription is activated in the app installed through your link.
- **AdGuard DNS:** when it’s made from an account registered through your link.
- **Promo codes:** when a customer pays with your promo code, even without a click on your link.

## Filters

Filters change the statistics and reports on the page:

- *Period*: *Today*, *Yesterday*, *7 days*, *This week*, *This month*, *Last month*, *30 days*, *90 days*, or *All time*
- *Country*: the country of the visitor or user
- *Subaccount*: the label you added to your links. See [Subaccounts](/tracking/subaccounts)
- *Product*: the app or service, such as AdGuard VPN for Android or AdGuard DNS

Some filters aren’t available in every report.

## Reports

In *Report by*, choose how to group your statistics:

- *By date*: statistics for each day. Click a date to see its statistics by hour.
- *By country*: where your visitors and customers are from
- *By subaccount*: results for each subaccount
- *By referral*: affiliates you invited and what they earned. See [Referral commission](/earnings/referral-commission)
- *By product*: results for each app and service
- *By source*: websites and platforms your visitors came from

To save a report, click *Export to CSV*.

## When new data appears

Visits appear right away. Installs, purchases, and revenue can take up to 48 hours.

## Statistics and your balance

*Revenue* in your statistics shows your earnings before payout fees. Refunds and chargebacks are deducted from your balance. That’s why your balance and payouts can be lower than your revenue in the statistics. See [Payout fees and currency](/earnings/payout-fees) and [Refunds and payout holds](/earnings/refunds-and-holds).

## Frequently asked questions

### What’s the difference between downloads and installations?

*Downloads* count every app downloaded through your link. *Installations* count only the apps that were then installed.

### Why do I see visits but no purchases?

People clicked your link but haven’t bought anything yet, or the sale isn’t credited to you for another reason. See [Why a sale wasn’t credited](/tracking/missing-conversions).

### Why don’t sales with my promo code appear under my subaccount?

Sales with promo codes aren’t tied to a subaccount. You’ll find them under *No subaccount*.

### What does Expirations mean?

It’s the number of trials and licenses that expired.
