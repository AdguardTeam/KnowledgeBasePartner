## When AdGuard affiliate payouts are made

AdGuard sends affiliate payouts once a week, on Thursdays. You request each payout yourself in the AdGuard Partner Portal, and we process it on the next payout day.

:::note

To request a payout, you need to add a [payout method](/earnings/payout-methods) first.

:::

## Payout schedule

- **Payout day:** every Thursday, between 00:01 and 23:59 GMT.
- **Request deadline:** Wednesday, 18:00 GMT. If you send your request later, we process it the following Thursday.
- **Time zone:** payouts follow Greenwich Mean Time (GMT), so the date may look different in your local time.

## Minimum payout

The minimum depends on your payout method. It’s set in your account currency: US dollars or euros.

| Payout method | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| PayPal | $20 or €20 |
| Mellow | $20 or €20 |
| Crypto wallet (USDT TRC20) | $50 or €50 |
| Wire transfer | $200 or €200 |

You’ll see the same amounts in the *Request payout* window.

## Maximum payout through Mellow

Mellow can send up to $10,000 in one payout, including the fee. If your balance is larger, we pay it in several parts. To avoid waiting, request payouts regularly so that your balance doesn’t grow above this limit.

## How to request a payout

1. In the left menu of the Partner Portal, click *Payouts*.

1. Click *Request payout*.

    <!-- Screenshot: ![Request payout window with the payout schedule, minimum amounts, and the amount available for withdrawal](/img/earnings-payout-schedule-request-payout.webp) -->

1. Check the amount under *Available for withdrawal* and the account that will receive the money.

1. Click *Send request*.

You’ll see *We got your request* and the date when we’ll send the money.

Keep in mind:

- You can’t cancel a request after you send it.
- Send one request at a time. Don’t send several requests at once.

## When referral commissions become available

Commissions from affiliates you invited become available for withdrawal one day after they’re added to your balance. See [Referral commission](/earnings/referral-commission).

## Fees

The payment system charges a fee when it sends your payout. The fee depends on your payout method. You’ll find the amount after fees in the *Net* column in *Payouts*. See [Payout fees and currency](/earnings/payout-fees).

## What can delay a payout

- You sent your request after 18:00 GMT on Wednesday.
- Your available balance is below the minimum for your payout method.
- It’s your first payout. Before the first payout, we may review your traffic sources and how you promote AdGuard.
- Your payout service is waiting for you. For example, Mellow pays only to verified accounts, and PayPal sometimes holds payments on its side.
- Your Mellow payout is over the limit, so we pay it in parts.
- Your account is under a compliance review. During a review, we may hold payouts for up to 90 days, or as long as we need to finish the review. See [Refunds and payout holds](/earnings/refunds-and-holds).

If none of these apply and your payout is late, [contact partner support](/support/contact-support).

## Frequently asked questions

### What day are AdGuard affiliate payouts made?

Every Thursday, between 00:01 and 23:59 GMT.

### What is the minimum payout?

$20 for PayPal and Mellow, $50 for a crypto wallet, and $200 for a wire transfer. If your account is in euros, the same amounts apply in euros.

### I sent my request on Wednesday evening. When will I get paid?

If you sent it before 18:00 GMT, we’ll send the money that Thursday. If you sent it later, we’ll send it the following Thursday.

### Can I cancel a payout request?

No. Once you send a request, it can’t be canceled. Check the amount and the account before you click *Send request*.

### Why can’t I send a payout request?

Make sure you’ve added a payout method and that the amount under *Available for withdrawal* is at least the minimum for your method. If both are fine, contact partner support: we may need to review your account first.
