## Earn 5% of the commission of affiliates you invite

Invite other people to join the AdGuard affiliate program. When they earn commissions, you get 5% of what they earn.

## How the referral commission works

People who sign up for the [affiliate program](/getting-started/sign-up-affiliate) through your referral link become your [referrals](/support/glossary#referral). You get 5% of the commission they earn, not 5% of the sale.

For example, your referral earns a $50 commission on a sale. You get 5% of $50, which is $2.50.

The referral program has only one level. You don’t earn on the referrals of your referrals.

## Where to find your referral link

1. In the left menu of the Partner Portal, click *Promo tools*.

1. In the *Referral link* block, find *Your referral link*.

1. Click the copy icon next to it.

Share the link with people who may want to promote AdGuard: other bloggers, content creators, or website owners.

## How to track your referrals

1. In the left menu of the Partner Portal, click *Affiliate statistics*.

1. In *Report by*, select *By referral*.

For each referral, you’ll see:

- *Username* and *Registration date*
- *Visits*, *Installations*, and *Registrations* from their links
- *Referral revenue*: what your referral earned
- *Your revenue*: your 5% of their earnings

## When you can withdraw referral commission

Referral commissions become available for withdrawal one day after they’re added to your balance. See [Payout schedule](/earnings/payout-schedule).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the referral commission 5% of the sale?

No. It’s 5% of the commission your referral earns from the sale.

### Do I earn on my referrals’ referrals?

No. The referral program has only one level.

### Where do I see how much I earned from referrals?

In *Affiliate statistics*, in the *By referral* report, in the *Your revenue* column.
