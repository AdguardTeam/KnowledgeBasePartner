## How we calculate your commission: VAT, fees, and discounts

Your commission is your rate multiplied by our revenue from the sale. Our revenue is not the full price: we first subtract VAT or other sales tax and the payment system fee.

## The formula

Commission = (price paid − VAT − payment system fee) × your rate

- **VAT or other sales tax.** The price that customers see already includes VAT in many countries. We subtract it first.
- **Payment system fee.** The payment system charges a fee for each payment. We calculate it on the full amount the customer paid, including VAT.
- **Your rate.** It depends on the product and your tier. See [Commission rates](/earnings/commission-rates).

## Example with VAT

A customer in the UK pays £114. The price includes 20% VAT. Your rate is 40%.

1. **Our revenue after VAT:** £114 / 1.2 = £95.

1. **Payment system fee:** for example, 3% of the full amount: £114 × 0.03 = £3.42.

1. **Base for your commission:** £95 − £3.42 = £91.58.

1. **Your commission:** £91.58 × 40% = £36.63.

## Example without VAT

A customer pays £114, and no VAT applies. Your rate is 40%.

1. **Payment system fee:** £114 × 0.03 = £3.42.

1. **Base for your commission:** £114 − £3.42 = £110.58.

1. **Your commission:** £110.58 × 40% = £44.23.

If a customer pays in another currency, we convert the amount at the market rate at the time of the payment.

## Promo codes and discounts

If a customer uses a promo code, we calculate everything from the discounted price: both the payment system fee and your commission. See [Promo codes](/tracking/promo-codes).

## Why the amount may differ from what you expected

The amount of your commission can change because of:

- The payment method the customer chose. Payment systems charge different fees.
- Promo codes or other discounts the customer used
- Promotions on our websites
- VAT or other sales tax in the customer’s country

## Refunds

If a customer gets a refund, we cancel the commission for that sale. See [Refunds and payout holds](/earnings/refunds-and-holds).

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is my commission less than my rate times the price?

Because we apply your rate to our revenue after VAT and the payment system fee, not to the full price.

### Do you subtract VAT for every country?

Only where VAT or another sales tax applies to the purchase.

### Is my commission on renewals calculated the same way?

Yes. Renewals are calculated the same way as new purchases.

### Does the payment system fee depend on the payment method?

Yes. Different payment methods have different fees, so your commission can differ for the same product.
