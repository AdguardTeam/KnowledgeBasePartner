## How much you earn: commission rates and tiers

Your commission is a share of our revenue from each sale and renewal. The rate depends on the product and on how much you earned in the previous month. You earn the same rates on new purchases and on renewals.

## Welcome Boost: your first 30 days

For 30 days after you sign up, you get our top rates on every product. No minimum volume is required.

| Product | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| AdGuard Ad Blocker | 70% |
| AdGuard VPN | 60% |
| AdGuard DNS Personal | 60% |
| AdGuard DNS Team | 40% |

See [Welcome Boost](/getting-started/welcome-boost).

## Rates after the Welcome Boost

After the Welcome Boost, your rates depend on your total earnings in the previous month:

| Total earnings last month, USD | Ad Blocker | VPN and DNS Personal | DNS Team |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $0–1,000 | 50% | 40% | 20% |
| $1,001–10,000 | 55% | 45% | 25% |
| $10,001–35,000 | 60% | 50% | 30% |
| $35,001–100,000 | 65% | 55% | 35% |
| $100,001+ | 70% | 60% | 40% |

Last updated: 14 Oct 2026

If you mostly promote AdGuard VPN, see the [AdGuard VPN affiliate program](https://adguardpartner.com/en/vpn-affiliate-program.html) page.

## How your tier is set

- Your tier is set automatically on the 1st of each month, based on your total earnings in the previous month.
- From the end of the Welcome Boost until the 1st of the next month, you get the rates of the first tier.
- Tiers are always calculated in US dollars. If your account is in euros, each sale is converted to US dollars at the exchange rate on the day of the sale.

For example, if you earned $12,000 in May, from 1 Jun you get 60% for AdGuard Ad Blocker, 50% for AdGuard VPN and AdGuard DNS Personal, and 30% for AdGuard DNS Team.

## Where to see your rates

In the left menu of the Partner Portal, click *Rates*. You’ll see the rates for the current month and the table of tiers. During the Welcome Boost, the page shows the date when it ends.

If the page says *Your commission rates are set manually by the manager*, your rates don’t change automatically. To switch to automatic tiers, [contact partner support](/support/contact-support).

## What the rate applies to

We apply your rate to our revenue from the sale: the price the customer paid, minus VAT or other sales tax and the payment system fee. See [How your commission is calculated](/earnings/commission-calculation).

On top of your own commission, you can get 5% of the commission earned by affiliates you invite. See [Referral commission](/earnings/referral-commission).

If you joined through Impact or Admitad, the Welcome Boost and these tiers don’t apply to you. See [Network affiliates](/getting-started/network-affiliates).

## Frequently asked questions

### When does my tier change?

On the 1st of each month. It’s based on your total earnings in the previous month.

### What rates do I get after the Welcome Boost ends?

Until the 1st of the next month, you get the rates of the first tier. After that, your rates depend on your earnings in the previous month.

### Do earnings in euros count toward my tier?

Yes. Each sale is converted to US dollars at the exchange rate on the day of the sale.

### Do I earn the same rate on renewals?

Yes. You earn the same rates on new purchases and on renewals.

### How can I get a higher rate?

Your rates go up automatically when your monthly earnings grow. Check the table of tiers to see how much you need to earn for the next tier.
