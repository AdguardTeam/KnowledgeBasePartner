## Payout methods: PayPal, Mellow, crypto, and wire transfer

To get paid, add a payout method in the AdGuard Partner Portal. You can choose PayPal, Mellow, or a crypto wallet yourself, and we can set up a wire transfer for you on request.

:::note

You can have one payout method at a time. Each method has its own minimum payout. See [Payout schedule](/earnings/payout-schedule).

:::

## Which method to choose

| Method | What you need | Paid in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PayPal | A PayPal account | US dollars |
| Mellow | A Mellow.io account. If you don’t have one, it will be created for you | US dollars |
| Crypto wallet | A USDT wallet on the TRON network (TRC20) | USDT |
| Wire transfer | Your bank details. Our team sets this method up for you | Ask partner support |

Payment systems charge their own fees. See [Payout fees and currency](/earnings/payout-fees).

## How to add PayPal

1. In the left menu of the Partner Portal, click *Payouts*.

1. Click *Add payout method*.

1. Select *PayPal*.

1. In *PayPal account*, enter the email address of your PayPal account. Use the email address, not a paypal.me link.

1. Click *Save*.

You’ll see *Payout method added*.

If a PayPal payment shows as unclaimed, accept it in your PayPal account. PayPal returns unclaimed payments after 30 days. If a payment fails, the money goes back to your balance, and you can request it again.

## How to add Mellow

Mellow is a payment service that pays to bank cards and other methods, such as Webmoney.

1. In the left menu of the Partner Portal, click *Payouts*.

1. Click *Add payout method*.

1. Select *Mellow.io* and click *Proceed*.

1. Enter the email address of your Mellow.io account. If you don’t have an account yet, enter the email address you want to use: we’ll create the account for you.

1. Click *Bind account*. By binding your account, you accept the Mellow.io Offer Agreement.

1. If we created a new Mellow.io account for you, use the link sent to your email address to add your payment details in Mellow.io.

1. Verify your Mellow.io account. Mellow pays only to verified accounts. If your account isn’t verified, you’ll see *Verify your Mellow.io account* in the Partner Portal. Click *Go to Mellow.io* and follow the instructions there.

Mellow has a maximum amount for one payout. If your balance is larger, we pay it in several parts. See [Payout schedule](/earnings/payout-schedule).

For questions about your Mellow.io account, such as verification or tax details, contact Mellow support.

## How to add a crypto wallet

We pay in USDT on the TRON network (TRC20). Other coins and networks aren’t supported.

1. In the left menu of the Partner Portal, click *Payouts*.

1. Click *Add payout method*.

1. Select *USDT*.

1. In *Crypto wallet*, enter your USDT TRC20 wallet address. It has 34 characters and starts with “T”.

1. Click *Save*.

You’ll see *You can now receive your rewards in USDT*.

:::caution

Crypto transfers can’t be reversed. Check the wallet address carefully. Use a wallet that accepts transfers from other people: some exchanges reject or freeze incoming transfers.

:::

## How to get paid by wire transfer

You can’t add a wire transfer in the Partner Portal yourself. Our team sets it up for you, and it appears in *Payouts* as *Custom*. Wire transfers are available to both individuals and companies.

To set it up, [contact partner support](/support/contact-support) and send:

- Whether you’re an individual, a sole proprietor, or a company
- Your full name or the company name
- Your address
- Your IBAN

## How to change or remove your payout method

1. In the left menu of the Partner Portal, click *Payouts*.

1. Click the pencil icon next to your current payout method.

1. In the *Change payout method* window, select a new method and enter its details, or click *Remove payout method*.

If you’re paid by wire transfer, contact partner support to change your payout method.

If you remove your payout method, you won’t be able to request payouts until you add a new one.

Changing the email address you use to log in to the Partner Portal doesn’t change your payout account. To get paid to another account, change your payout method.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which payout method is the fastest?

All methods are processed on the same day: every Thursday. The time it takes for the money to reach you depends on the payment system. See [Payout schedule](/earnings/payout-schedule).

### Can I get paid to a bank card?

Yes, through Mellow. Add Mellow as your payout method, and then add your card in your Mellow.io account.

### Can I get paid in Bitcoin or another cryptocurrency?

No. We pay only in USDT on the TRON network (TRC20).

### I entered the wrong wallet address. Can you get my money back?

Crypto transfers can’t be reversed, so we can’t return money sent to a wrong address. Check the address before you request a payout.

### Why does the Partner Portal say it can’t find my Mellow account?

The email address you entered doesn’t match an account in the AdGuard project on Mellow.io. If you changed your email address in Mellow, update it in the Partner Portal too.
