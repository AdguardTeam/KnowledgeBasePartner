You earn commission only on genuine, final sales. If a customer gets their money back, we cancel the commission for that sale. We can also hold payouts while we review an account.

## When we cancel a commission

We cancel the commission and deduct it from your balance if the purchase is:

- Refunded, canceled, or returned
- Reversed by a chargeback: the customer asked their bank or card issuer to return the payment
- Found to be fraudulent or made in breach of the [Terms and Conditions](https://adguardpartner.com/en/terms-and-conditions.html)

We also don’t pay commission on ineligible orders, such as:

- Self-referrals: purchases made through your own link for yourself, your family, your associates, or your business
- Fake orders or orders that aren’t made in good faith
- Orders with coupons that we didn’t issue to you or publish ourselves
- Other orders that break the Terms and Conditions

## Refunds after a payout and negative balance

A customer can get a refund after we’ve already paid you for the sale. In this case, we deduct the commission from your balance. If your balance isn’t enough, it can become negative.

While your balance is negative, we deduct the amount from your new commissions until it’s covered. You can request a payout again when your available balance reaches the minimum for your payout method. See [Payout schedule](/earnings/payout-schedule).

That’s also why your balance can be lower than your earnings in *Affiliate statistics*: refunds and chargebacks are deducted from your balance.

## Payout holds

We can hold payouts while we review an account. During the review, you keep earning commissions, but we don’t pay them out.

We may hold your payouts if we suspect:

- A breach of the Terms and Conditions, for example, after another partner reports that you copied their content
- Fraud
- Problems with traffic quality
- Attempts to manipulate tracking

A hold can last up to 90 days, or as long as we need to finish the review. After the review, we release the commissions, reduce them, or cancel them, in full or in part.

Before your first payout, we may also review your traffic sources and how you promote AdGuard.

There’s no fixed holding period for commissions. Sales can take up to 48 hours to appear in your statistics, and referral commissions become available for withdrawal one day after they’re added to your balance.

## What to do if your payout is on hold

1. Check your email and your tickets in *Support*. If we need information from you, we’ll ask for it there.

1. Reply within three business days.

1. Send what we ask for. For example: your traffic sources, links to the pages where you placed your links, screenshots of your ads, and your campaign settings.

1. If we point out a problem, fix it and tell us when it’s done.

If you don’t reply or don’t cooperate, we may suspend or close your account and withhold your payouts until the review is over.

:::caution

If we close your account for a serious breach of the Terms and Conditions, you lose the commissions earned while you broke the rules or through prohibited methods. This includes commissions that we’ve already paid. See [Prohibited practices](/rules/prohibited-practices).

:::

## How to avoid reversals and holds

- Promote AdGuard only in the ways the Terms and Conditions allow. See [Allowed traffic sources](/rules/allowed-traffic-sources).
- Don’t buy AdGuard products through your own link or code.
- Describe AdGuard products accurately, so customers get what they expect. See [Product claims](/rules/product-claims).
- Use only promo codes that we issued to you or published ourselves.
- Reply to our questions quickly.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I lose my commission if a customer gets a refund?

Yes. We cancel the commission for a refunded sale and deduct it from your balance.

### Why did my balance go down?

Most often, because a customer got a refund or a payment was reversed by a chargeback. If you can’t find the reason, [contact partner support](/support/contact-support).

### Why is my payout on hold?

We may be reviewing your account, or it’s your first payout and we’re checking your traffic sources. Check your tickets in *Support* and reply to our questions within three business days.

### How long can a payout hold last?

Up to 90 days, or as long as we need to finish the review.

### Do I keep earning while my payout is on hold?

Yes. Commissions keep coming to your balance during the review. After the review, we release them, reduce them, or cancel them, depending on the result.
