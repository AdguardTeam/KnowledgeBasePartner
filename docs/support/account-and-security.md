You manage your account in *My profile* in the [AdGuard Partner Portal](/getting-started/partner-portal-overview). There, you can change your email address and password, turn on two-factor authentication, and delete your account.

:::note

Your Partner Portal account is separate from the AdGuard account you may use for AdGuard products. You can have only one affiliate account, unless we’ve agreed otherwise in writing.

:::

## How to change your email address

1. In the left menu of the Partner Portal, click *My profile*.

1. Next to *Email*, click *Change*.

1. Enter your new email address and your current password, and click *Change email*.

You’ll use the new address to log in. It doesn’t change your payout account. To get paid to another account, change your payout method. See [Payout methods](/earnings/payout-methods).

## How to change your password

1. In *My profile*, next to *Password*, click *Change*.

1. Enter your current password and the new password twice.

1. Confirm the change.

Your password must have at least eight characters. We don’t accept passwords that appear in public databases of leaked passwords.

If you forgot your password, click *Forgot password?* on the login page, enter your email address, and click *Reset password*.

## Two-factor authentication

With two-factor authentication (2FA), you enter a code each time you log in and when you make sensitive changes to your account. You can choose one of two methods:

- *One-time email code*: we send a code to your email address.
- *Code from authenticator app*: you use the current code from an app such as Google Authenticator.

### How to turn on 2FA with an authenticator app

1. In *My profile*, select *Code from authenticator app*.

1. Install an authenticator app on your phone and scan the QR code.

1. Save your setup key in a secure place. You’ll need it to link your account to a new authenticator app.

1. Enter the code from the app.

1. Optional: select *Log out of all devices*.

1. Click *Enable*.

1. Save your backup codes in a secure place.

Backup codes let you log in if you can’t open your authenticator app. Each code works once. You won’t see them again after you close the window.

### How to turn on 2FA with email codes

1. In *My profile*, select *One-time email code*.

1. Enter the code we sent to your email address.

1. Click *Enable*.

To switch to another 2FA method, turn off the current one first.

## If you can’t log in

- If you see *We temporarily blocked your account for safety reasons*, wait 15 minutes and try again.
- If you see that there are too many requests from your IP address, try again in a few minutes.
- If you lost access to your authenticator app, use one of your backup codes.
- If nothing helps, [contact partner support](/support/contact-support).

## How to delete your account

1. Request a payout of your remaining balance first. After you delete your account, its balance is blocked, and you can’t request payouts.

1. In *My profile*, click *Delete account*.

1. Enter your password and confirm the deletion.

We delete all information from your account, including your email address. You won’t be able to log in, and you’ll stop getting our emails. This can’t be undone.

If you’re a reseller, email us at [b2b@adguard.com](mailto:b2b@adguard.com) to delete your account.

## Frequently asked questions

### Will changing my email address change where I get paid?

No. Your payout method stays the same. To get paid to another account, change your payout method.

### Can I have two affiliate accounts?

Only with our written permission.

### I lost my phone with the authenticator app. What do I do?

Log in with one of your backup codes. To link your account to a new authenticator app, use your setup key. If you don’t have them, contact partner support.
