## How to contact partner support

The fastest way to reach us is a support ticket in the AdGuard Partner Portal. You can also email us: affiliates and resellers have different addresses.

## Create a support ticket

1. In the left menu of the Partner Portal, click *Support*.

1. Click *New ticket*.

1. In *Summary*, describe your question in a few words.

1. In *Description*, add the details. See what to include below.

1. Click *Send*.

You’ll see *Ticket #… created*. We’ll reply in the ticket, and you’ll find all your tickets in *Support*. When your question is solved, you can close the ticket. If you have something to add later, reopen it.

If you can’t create a ticket, email us instead.

## Email addresses

- **Affiliates:** [affiliate@adguard.com](mailto:affiliate@adguard.com)
- **Resellers:** [b2b@adguard.com](mailto:b2b@adguard.com)

Send your email from the address you use to log in to the Partner Portal. This helps us find your account.

## What to include

| Question | What to send |
| --- | --- |
| A sale wasn’t credited | The email address the customer used at checkout, the order or receipt number, the date of the purchase, and the link the customer used. See [Why a sale wasn’t credited](/tracking/missing-conversions) |
| A payout is late | The date of your payout request and your payout method. See [Payout schedule](/earnings/payout-schedule) |
| A promo code doesn’t work | The code and a screenshot of the error |
| Approval for a campaign | The channel, its audience, the ad format, and links to examples. See [Allowed traffic sources](/rules/allowed-traffic-sources) |
| A postback doesn’t work | Your postback URL and the click IDs of the missing conversions |
| An invoice | The dates and amounts of the payouts, and your details. See [Invoices and taxes](/earnings/invoices-and-taxes) |

Ask one question per ticket. It helps us answer faster.

:::caution

Never send your password or two-factor authentication codes to anyone, including us.

:::

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I contact you on Telegram or WhatsApp?

Please use tickets in the Partner Portal or email. That’s how we keep track of every request.

### I can’t log in. How do I contact you?

Email us from the address of your Partner Portal account: [affiliate@adguard.com](mailto:affiliate@adguard.com) for affiliates or [b2b@adguard.com](mailto:b2b@adguard.com) for resellers.

### Where do I see the answer to my ticket?

In *Support* in the Partner Portal.
