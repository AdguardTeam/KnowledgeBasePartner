## Glossary of AdGuard Partner Program terms

Here are the terms you’ll see in the AdGuard Partner Portal and in this knowledge base, in alphabetical order.

## A–C

### Account currency

The currency of your balance and minimum payouts: US dollars or euros. You choose it when you sign up. See [Payout fees and currency](/earnings/payout-fees).

### AdGuard Partner Portal

The online dashboard where you manage your participation in the AdGuard Partner Program. See [Partner Portal overview](/getting-started/partner-portal-overview).

### AdGuard Partner Program

Our partnership program with two parts: the affiliate program and the reseller program.

### Affiliate

A partner who promotes AdGuard products and earns a commission on sales and renewals from referred customers.

### Affiliate link

A link with your partner ID. It tells us that a visitor came from you. See [Affiliate links](/tracking/affiliate-links).

### Brand bidding

Bidding on AdGuard brand names in paid search ads. It’s prohibited. See [Brand bidding](/rules/brand-bidding).

### Chargeback

A payment that the customer’s bank returns to the customer at their request. We cancel the commission for it.

### Click ID

A unique ID that your tracker gives to each click. You pass it to us in the `externalid` parameter, and we send it back in postbacks.

### Commission

Your share of our revenue from a sale or a renewal.

### Cookie

A small file that our website saves in the visitor’s browser after a click on your link. It gives the visitor 365 days to install the app or sign up to be linked to you. See [How sales are credited](/tracking/cookie-window).

## D–P

### Deep link

An affiliate link to a specific page of our website, not to the home page. See [Deep links](/tracking/deep-links).

### Disclosure

A note telling your audience that you earn a commission. It’s required wherever you share affiliate links. See [Affiliate disclosure](/rules/disclosure).

### Distributor API

An API for resellers to generate and manage license keys from their own systems. See [Reseller API](/reseller/reseller-api).

### Downloads

Apps downloaded through your link, whether or not they were installed later.

### Event type

The type of conversion in a postback: `INSTALL`, `TRIAL`, or `SALE`.

### Expirations

Trials and licenses that expired.

### Installation link

A link that downloads the app with your partner ID inside.

### Installations

Installs of apps downloaded through your link.

### Last click

The rule for crediting sales: the last click before the install or sign-up counts.

### Minimum payout

The smallest amount you can request. It depends on your payout method.

### Net

The amount of a payout after the payment system fee.

### Partner ID

Your unique number in the affiliate program. It’s in all your links: after `aid=` or after `/d/`.

### Payout hold

A pause in payouts while we review an account. It can last up to 90 days, or as long as we need to finish the review. See [Refunds and payout holds](/earnings/refunds-and-holds).

### Payout method

The way you get paid: PayPal, Mellow, a USDT crypto wallet, or a wire transfer.

### Postback

A request that our server sends to your server when a conversion happens. See [How postbacks work](/postbacks/how-postbacks-work).

### Postpaid

A reseller billing model: you generate licenses when you need them and pay one invoice for the period. See [Prepaid vs postpaid](/reseller/prepaid-vs-postpaid).

### Prepaid

A reseller billing model: you pay for each order before we generate the licenses.

### Promo code

A discount code tied to your account. Sales with it are credited to you. See [Promo codes](/tracking/promo-codes).

### Promo page

A landing page that shows the benefits of an AdGuard product. You can add your promo code to it.

### Purchases

Purchases that earn you a commission.

## R–Z

### Referral

An affiliate who joined the program through your referral link. You get 5% of their commission. See [Referral commission](/earnings/referral-commission).

### Registrations

Sign-ups on our websites and in our apps through your link.

### Renewals

Renewals and upgrades of licenses, automatic and manual. You earn on them too.

### Replacement key

A free new license key that a prepaid reseller gets after blocking a license that wasn’t activated or was activated within the last 30 days.

### Reseller

A partner who buys AdGuard licenses at a discount and sells them to their own customers.

### Revenue

Your earnings for the selected period, based on your rate.

### Self-referral

A purchase made through your own link for yourself, your family, your associates, or your business. It’s prohibited.

### Subaccount

A label you add to your link to see which channel brings results. See [Subaccounts](/tracking/subaccounts).

### Tier

The level of your commission rates. It’s set on the 1st of each month, based on your earnings in the previous month.

### Variable

A placeholder in curly braces in your postback URL, such as `{externalid}`. We replace it with a real value. See [Postback parameters](/postbacks/postback-parameters).

### Visits

Clicks on your link from unique IP addresses.

### Welcome Boost

Our top commission rates for your first 30 days in the affiliate program. See [Welcome Boost](/getting-started/welcome-boost).

### White label

A technology partnership in which a company builds AdGuard technology into its own branded product with our SDK. It’s not reselling. For a VPN, see [white-label VPN](https://adguardpartner.com/en/white-label-vpn.html).

### Zero invoice

An invoice with a zero amount that we create for each license generation on the postpaid model, to keep track of it.
