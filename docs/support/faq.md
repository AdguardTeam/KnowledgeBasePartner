Short answers to the questions partners ask us most often. Each answer links to an article with the details. For the meaning of program terms, see the [Glossary](/support/glossary).

## Getting started

### How does the AdGuard affiliate program work?

You share your affiliate links or promo code, and you earn a commission on every sale and renewal from customers you referred. See [How the program works](/getting-started/how-the-program-works).

### Do I need a website to join?

No. You can also promote AdGuard on a blog, a YouTube or Telegram channel, or social media.

### Do I need approval to join?

No. Your account opens as soon as you confirm your email address. Before your first payout, we may review your traffic sources. See [How to sign up](/getting-started/sign-up-affiliate).

### What commission do you pay?

Up to 70% of our revenue from each sale and renewal, depending on the product and your monthly earnings. For your first 30 days, you get our top rates. See [Commission rates](/earnings/commission-rates). If you mostly promote AdGuard VPN, see the [AdGuard VPN affiliate program](https://adguardpartner.com/vpn-affiliate-program.html).

### I see clicks and installs but no commission. Do you pay per install?

No. We pay only for sales and renewals, not for clicks, installs, or sign-ups.

## Tracking and promo codes

### How long does the cookie last?

365 days. After a click on your link, the person has one year to install the app or sign up to be linked to you. See [How sales are credited](/tracking/cookie-window).

### Is a sale credited if the customer used my promo code but never clicked my link?

Yes. Sales with your promo code are always credited to you.

### The customer clicked my link, but the sale went to another partner. Why?

After clicking your link, the customer may have clicked another partner’s link before installing the app. They may also have installed the app earlier through another partner’s link, or paid with another partner’s promo code. See [Why a sale wasn’t credited](/tracking/missing-conversions).

### Can I get promo codes for my site or channel, and how long are they valid?

Yes. Request a code in *Promo tools* → *Promo codes*. Each code works for six months. See [Promo codes](/tracking/promo-codes).

### My promo code expired. Can you extend it?

No, but you can request a new code. Replace the old code everywhere you published it.

### Where do users enter the promo code on iPhone or Android?

On our website, at checkout: promo codes work only there. AdGuard apps for iOS don’t have a promo code field. After the purchase, customers log in to the app with the same email address.

### Do purchases in the App Store and Google Play count?

Yes, if the app was installed through your link. See [Platforms and apps](/tracking/platforms-and-apps).

### Why don’t I see my sales right away?

Visits appear right away, but installs, purchases, and earnings can take up to 48 hours.

## Payouts

### When are payouts made, and what is the minimum?

Every Thursday, on your request. The minimum depends on your payout method. See [Payout schedule](/earnings/payout-schedule).

### Can I be paid by bank transfer or in USDT instead of PayPal?

Yes. You can add a USDT wallet on the TRON network yourself. For a wire transfer, contact partner support. See [Payout methods](/earnings/payout-methods).

### PayPal is holding my payout or has rejected it. Can you release it?

Holds on PayPal’s side are managed by PayPal, so contact PayPal support. If a payout fails, the money goes back to your balance, and you can request it again.

### How do I change my PayPal email or crypto wallet?

In *Payouts*, click the pencil icon next to your payout method. See [Payout methods](/earnings/payout-methods).

### Are there any payout fees?

Yes. Payment systems charge fees that depend on the payout method. See [Payout fees and currency](/earnings/payout-fees).

### Why is my payout on hold, and how long will it last?

We may be reviewing your account. A hold can last up to 90 days, or as long as we need to finish the review. Reply to our questions within three business days. See [Refunds and payout holds](/earnings/refunds-and-holds).

### Can you issue invoices for my payouts?

Yes. We issue a separate invoice for each payout on request. See [Invoices and taxes](/earnings/invoices-and-taxes).

## Rules

### Can I bid on “AdGuard” keywords in Google Ads?

No. Bidding on AdGuard brand names and their variations is prohibited. See [Brand bidding](/rules/brand-bidding).

### Can I post my promo code on coupon websites?

Not on third-party coupon or deal websites. A coupon website that you own is allowed after our written approval. See [Coupon and deal websites](/rules/coupon-and-deal-sites).

### Do I need approval to promote AdGuard on social media or Telegram?

Posts in your own channels don’t need approval. Ads in other people’s channels and ads on social media need our written approval first. See [Allowed traffic sources](/rules/allowed-traffic-sources).

## Licenses and reselling

### Can I get a free license to test and review the products?

Yes. Request a personal license in a support ticket. See [Personal license](/getting-started/personal-license).

### I want to resell AdGuard licenses. What’s the minimum order, and is postpaid possible?

The minimum order is 50 licenses. Postpaid is available to large partners with regular sales. See [Prepaid vs postpaid](/reseller/prepaid-vs-postpaid). To resell AdGuard VPN, see the [AdGuard VPN reseller program](https://adguardpartner.com/vpn-reseller-program.html).

### Can I sell AdGuard VPN under my own brand?

Not as a reseller. To build AdGuard VPN technology into your own product, see [white-label VPN](https://adguardpartner.com/white-label-vpn.html).
